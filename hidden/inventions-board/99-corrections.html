  • Three sparks, "Shadow gate", "Swing scale for hidden mass" and "Heat-map paper", are taken down. Each drew on a journal headline whose paper had not been opened, so the people behind the work were not credited. They come back only once the paper is read and credited by author and title. Five other sparks said they were "the owner's idea", although each rests on an established principle that others discovered; that line is gone until each principle is credited to its source.
