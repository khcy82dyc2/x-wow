Whose shoulders SWELL stands onSWELL 站在谁的肩膀上

SWELL invents very little. It combines other people's published work into one box meant to be cheap. Here is who we took from, and what we took. Every entry comes from the sources already cited in the SWELL files, apart from the two differential phase contrast papers, added on 12 October 2026, when that credit was found missing.

SWELL 自己发明的东西很少，它是把别人已经发表的成果拼进一个以低成本为目标的盒子里。下面列出我们向谁借了什么。除了两篇差分相衬论文（2026 年 10 月 12 日补上，因为一次 AI 审查发现漏了这份致谢），每一条都来自 SWELL 文件里已经引用过的出处。

Science科学

  • Fei Chen, Paul Tillberg & Edward Boyden, 2015, Science Expansion microscopy itself: swell the sample, not the optics. Their distortion analysis became SWELL's rule that every gel checks its own expansion. 膨胀显微技术本身：放大样品，而不是放大光学系统。他们的形变分析，成了 SWELL"每块凝胶自己检验膨胀"的规矩。 doi:10.1126/science.1260088
  • Paul Tillberg et al., 2016, Nature Biotechnology Protein-retention ExM (proExM) and AcX anchoring: how proteins get tied to the gel. 蛋白保留型 ExM（proExM）和 AcX 锚定：蛋白质怎样被拴在凝胶上。 doi:10.1038/nbt.3625
  • Yongxin Zhao et al., 2017, Nature Biotechnology Expansion pathology (ExPath) on clinical specimens: evidence that the chemistry works on real tissue. 临床标本上的膨胀病理（ExPath）：证明这套化学在真实组织上行得通。 doi:10.1038/nbt.3892
  • Davide Gambarotto et al., 2019, Nature Methods Ultrastructure ExM (U-ExM): the ~4× heat-and-SDS gel family SWELL starts with. They tuned it with the centriole as a molecular ruler, an early intrinsic ruler. 超微结构 ExM（U-ExM）：SWELL 起步用的约 4 倍、加热加 SDS 变性的凝胶体系。他们用中心粒当分子标尺来调这套方法，这是较早的内在标尺。 doi:10.1038/s41592-018-0238-1
  • Mikus, Dudin & Dey, "U-ExM of diverse samples", protocols.io, 2025 The starting point for SWELL's wet-lab recipe (not written up yet): monomer mix, stock solutions and timings. SWELL 湿实验配方的起点（还没写出来）：单体混合液、母液和时间安排。 doi:10.17504/protocols.io.261gee4qwg47/v1
  • "Neutralising acrylic acid to make sodium acrylate", protocols.io (authors as listed there)"Neutralising acrylic acid to make sodium acrylate"，protocols.io（作者见原页面） The fallback for making sodium acrylate in a lab, in a fume hood only. 在实验室里自制丙烯酸钠的备用方案（只能在通风橱里做）。 protocols.io
  • Ons M'Saad & Joerg Bewersdorf, 2020, Nature Communications Pan-ExM and the NHS-ester total-protein stain that gives EM-like contrast. Pan-ExM 和 NHS 酯全蛋白染色，带来类似电镜的反差。 doi:10.1038/s41467-020-17523-8
  • Hugo Damstra et al., 2022, eLife 11:e73775, and its 2022 correction Ten-fold robust ExM (TREx): SWELL's 10× track. The correction taught us to check the TEMED stock concentration. 十倍稳健 ExM（TREx）：SWELL 的 10 倍路线。那份更正让我们学会核对 TEMED 母液浓度。 eLife 73775 · correction更正
  • Yu Shrike Zhang, Jae-Byum Chang et al., 2016, Scientific Reports 6:22691 Expansion mini-microscopy: expansion paired with a webcam mini-microscope, at a hundredth to a thousandth of a benchtop microscope's price. SWELL's first idea, "grow the sample, not the glass", is theirs. 膨胀迷你显微：把膨胀显微和网络摄像头迷你显微镜配在一起，价格只有台式显微镜的百分之一到千分之一。SWELL 的第一个想法"放大样本，而不是升级镜头"，是他们先提出的。 doi:10.1038/srep22691
  • Thomas Sheard, Izzy Jayasinghe et al., 2019, ACS Nano 13:2143 Sarcomere periodicity and the fixed width of Z-discs used as intrinsic reporters of local gel expansion, in heart cells. SWELL's muscle ruler is their idea; in our dated searches (docs/design/prior_art.md in the download, 10 October 2026) we found nothing that pairs each myofibril's label-free period before expansion with its own period after. 在心肌细胞里，用肌节周期和 Z 盘的固定宽度作为凝胶局部膨胀的内在指示。SWELL 的肌肉标尺是他们的想法；在我们有日期的检索里（下载包中的 docs/design/prior_art.md，2026 年 10 月 10 日），没有找到把每根肌原纤维膨胀前的免标记周期和它自己膨胀后的周期配对的做法。 doi:10.1021/acsnano.8b08742
  • Thomas Sheard, 2021, University of Leeds PhD thesis利兹大学博士学位论文 Compared the sarcomere expansion factor with the gel's and found it lower: why this page says a myofibril's expansion may not be the gel's. 把肌节算出的膨胀倍数和凝胶的比较，发现前者更低：所以本页说，肌原纤维的膨胀不一定等于凝胶的膨胀。 White Rose eTheses
  • Luca Pesce, Paolo Bianchini et al., 2019, Journal of Biophotonics Nuclear pore complexes as an intrinsic reporter of expansion, from the macro- to the nanoscale. 用核孔复合体作为膨胀的内在指示，从宏观尺度一直到纳米尺度。 doi:10.1002/jbio.201900018
  • Marisa Vanheusden, Johan Hofkens et al., 2020, ACS Omega Photobleached 3D features as an intrinsic ruler for the expansion factor and for distortions. 用光漂白出的三维图案作为内在标尺，量膨胀倍数和形变。 doi:10.1021/acsomega.0c00118
  • Chozinski et al., 2016, Nature Methods Distortion measured on the same cells before and after expansion: generally below 100 nm (root mean square) over up to 30 µm. 在同一批细胞的膨胀前后测量形变：在 30 µm 以内的尺度上，均方根形变一般低于 100 nm。 doi:10.1038/nmeth.3833
  • Miao, Lin, Qian & Wei, 2022, Journal of Visualized Experiments (VISTA) Label-free images of the same structures before and after expansion, compared to read the expansion factor. SWELL's label-free before-image is not new; we found no use of a widefield, per-fibril period like SWELL's in our dated searches. 比较同一结构膨胀前后的免标记图像来读出膨胀倍数。SWELL 的免标记膨胀前图像并不新；在我们有日期的检索里，没有找到像 SWELL 这样用宽场显微逐根纤维读周期的做法。 doi:10.3791/63824
  • Shalin B. Mehta & Colin J. R. Sheppard, 2009, Optics Letters 34(13):1924–1926 Asymmetric illumination-based differential phase contrast (AIDPC): a full-field phase-gradient imaging method, shown on an optical fibre and a histochemically prepared skeletal-muscle section. SWELL's label-free before-image builds on this kind of differential phase contrast. Read from its abstract, 12 October 2026. 基于非对称照明的差分相衬（AIDPC）：一种全视场的相位梯度成像方法，演示样品是一根光纤和一张经组织化学处理的骨骼肌切片。SWELL 的免标记膨胀前图像建立在这类差分相衬之上。依据其摘要，2026 年 10 月 12 日读取。 doi:10.1364/OL.34.001924
  • Lei Tian & Laura Waller, 2015, Optics Express 23(9):11394–11403, and its 2023 erratum Quantitative differential phase contrast in an LED array microscope: phase recovered from pairs of images taken with asymmetric illumination, with several illumination angles for more stable phase recovery. SWELL's four oblique LEDs use this multi-angle, LED-based approach. A 2023 erratum (Optics Express 31(7):11804) corrects typos in the paper's derived transfer functions. Read from the abstracts, 12 October 2026. 在 LED 阵列显微镜上做定量差分相衬：用非对称照明拍的成对图像恢复相位，多个照明角度让相位恢复更稳定。SWELL 的四个斜射 LED 用的就是这种多角度、基于 LED 的思路。2023 年的一份勘误（Optics Express 31(7):11804）更正了论文推导出的传递函数里的笔误。依据摘要，2026 年 10 月 12 日读取。 doi:10.1364/OE.23.011394 · erratum勘误
  • Scheible & Tinnefeld, 2018, bioRxiv preprintbioRxiv 预印本 DNA-origami nanorulers proposed as measuring references inside the gel. 提出把 DNA 折纸纳米尺放进凝胶里，当作测量参照。 doi:10.1101/265405
  • Hugo Damstra, Lukas Kapitein et al., 2023, Nature Methods (GelMap) A fluorescent grid in the gel that expands with the sample: intrinsic calibration and deformation maps without cellular landmarks. SWELL's whole-gel strain map belongs to this family. 在凝胶里放一张荧光网格，随样品一起膨胀：不靠细胞结构就能校准倍数、画出形变图。SWELL 的整块凝胶应变图属于这一类方法。 doi:10.1038/s41592-023-02001-y
  • Francesca Soglia, Massimiliano Petracci & Eero Puolanne, 2020, Italian Journal of Animal Science Chicken-breast sarcomere lengths by laser diffraction: the reference for the chicken ruler and for the laser experiment. 用激光衍射测的鸡胸肉肌节长度：鸡肉标尺和激光小实验的参考值。 doi:10.1080/1828051X.2020.1761271
  • Collin Douglas et al., 2024, PLoS ONE Z-disc widths of 30–140 nm, depending on the muscle and species: a Z-disc could read out resolution once chicken breast's own Z-disc width is known. Z 盘宽 30–140 纳米，因肌肉和物种而异：等鸡胸肉自己的 Z 盘宽度知道了，Z 盘才能用来读出分辨率。 doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0300348
  • Migliore, 2022, University of North Texas thesis北德克萨斯大学学位论文 Prior art: expanded isolated rabbit myofibrils, imaged without labels in transmitted light, with the A-band as the expansion ruler. Muscle can be expanded, and measured from its own structure. 现有工作：膨胀分离的兔肌原纤维，用透射光不染色成像，并以 A 带作为膨胀的尺子。肌肉可以膨胀，也能用它自己的结构来量。 UNT Digital Library
