How SWELL was made

Made with AI from a person's brief; not built, and not yet checked by a person

SWELL was designed, simulated and documented with AI (Claude, made by Anthropic), working from the project owner's brief and orders. The AI wrote the audit, the simulation code, the documents, and this website's text and code.

What that means for you

  • simulated Every SWELL result is from simulation until someone builds it. Nothing was measured on a real machine.
  • You can check everything: the download holds the audit, the decisions log, the fact-check table with its sources, the simulation code and its outputs. Rerun the code and compare.
  • An AI can be confidently wrong.

How the work was kept honest

  • Every number from code. Numbers in generated documents are computed from data or a cited source.
  • Every claim tied to a check. A claim must be bound to a passing quality-control metric.
  • Facts with verdicts. Each key fact in the project notes records how far it could be checked against its source (in full, in part, or not yet), with the source (pm/VERIFIED_FACTS.md).
  • Never an author. In any paper, the AI is disclosed as the journal requires and never listed as an author.
