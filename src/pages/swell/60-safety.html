Safety安全

The chemistry is the dangerous part危险的是化学部分

Wet work only in a proper lab with a fume hood. Nothing chemical at home.湿实验只在带通风橱的正规实验室里做。家里不碰任何化学品。

Several reagents are carcinogens, mutagens or reproductive toxicants (CMR substances). Not in a kitchen, a garage or a spare room. Follow the host lab's induction and risk assessments. Each chemical's hazard below is read from a named supplier's safety data sheet (SDS), cited in its row and listed under the table; raw chicken has no sheet. Suppliers' sheets differ and change, so check the one for the product you use. This is a starting list, not a risk assessment.

好几种试剂会致癌、致突变或有生殖毒性（CMR 物质）。厨房、车库、空房间都不行。遵守所在实验室的入门培训和风险评估。下面每种化学品的危害都读自一家具名供应商的安全数据表（SDS），出处写在该行里，并列在表下；生鸡肉没有 SDS。各家的 SDS 不尽相同，也会更新，所以要核对你所用产品的那一份。这只是起步清单，不是风险评估。

The law (UK, as the project's notes read it)法规（英国，按项目笔记的理解）

UK REACH Annex XVII (entries 28–30) means CMR substances can't be supplied to the general public. This covers acrylamide and, by Reg. (EU) 2018/675 as retained in UK law, formaldehyde. This law doesn't restrict the instrument itself, but the chemistry needs professional-user access, and it belongs only in an equipped, supervised laboratory working under its own safety rules. Never misrepresent yourself to a supplier.

英国 REACH 法规附件 XVII（第 28–30 条）规定 CMR 物质不得供应给普通公众。这包括丙烯酰胺，以及经 (EU) 2018/675 号条例纳入、并保留在英国法律中的甲醛。这条法规不限制仪器本身，但化学部分需要专业用户渠道，而且只能在设备齐全、有人监督、按自身安全规程运作的实验室里做。绝不能向供应商谎报身份。

Hazardous waste goes through a licensed route with records; nothing hazardous goes down the drain or into household bins. Research use only: no diagnostic claims, no human material, no live animals. This is a summary, not legal advice; check the current rules where you are.

危险废物走有资质的渠道并留存记录；任何危险物都不进下水道、不进生活垃圾。仅限科研用途：不做任何诊断声明，不用人体材料，不用活体动物。这只是摘要，不是法律意见；请以你所在地的现行法规为准。

Never bypassed, in code or in design无论代码还是设计，绝不绕过

  • Lid interlock: opening the lid cuts the excitation LED and stops every motor (the steppers and the pumps) in hardware.盖子联锁：一开盖，硬件直接切断激发 LED，并让所有电机（步进电机和水泵）停下。
  • Moving parts: the stepper gears sit under fixed printed covers and the pumps' roller heads are enclosed, so fingers and hair can't be caught.活动部件：步进电机的齿轮装在固定的打印护罩里，水泵的滚轮头有外壳罩住，手指和头发不会被夹住。
  • Leak cutoff: a leak trip cuts the pumps, any heater and the LED through a hardware latch that firmware can't clear.漏液切断：一旦检测到漏液，硬件锁存电路切断水泵、加热器和 LED，固件无法解除。
  • Thermal cutoff: any heater gets a hardware cutoff rated ≤ {{n:swell.t_cutoff}} near printed parts. Version 1 has no heater on the stage at all.过热切断：任何加热器都要有硬件温控切断，靠近打印件的额定不超过 {{n:swell.t_cutoff}}。第一版载物台上干脆没有加热器。
  • No override: software never overrides these; there are no "force" flags.不许越权：软件永远不能覆盖以上保护，没有任何“强制”开关。
  • Low voltage: ≤ {{n:swell.v_max}} inside, from certified external supplies with fused rails. Never wire mains.低电压：机内不超过 {{n:swell.v_max}}，用认证的外置电源，各路带保险丝。绝不接市电。
  • Liquids: only the sealed sample vessel sits over the objective, with a drip ring, a sleeve and a leak sensor; no reservoirs above the optics or electronics.液体：物镜上方只放密封的样品容器，配接水环、护套和漏液传感器；光学和电子部件上方不放任何储液瓶。
  • Stage rules: no CMR liquids, no gel casting and no {{n:swell.t_denature}} steps on the stage.载物台规矩：台上不放 CMR 液体、不灌胶、不做 {{n:swell.t_denature}} 步骤。
  • Light: the high-power excitation LED runs only inside the closed box, and opening the lid cuts it; the white and oblique LEDs, which the interlock doesn't cover, must be ones their makers rate safe to look into. Never look into the excitation LED. No lasers and no ultraviolet source in the reference build. The laser pointer in the family experiment is separate from the instrument and has its own safety box.光：大功率激发 LED 只在密闭的盒子里工作，一开盖就被切断；白光和斜照 LED 不在联锁范围内，必须选厂商认定可以直视的型号。绝不直视激发 LED。参考设计里没有激光，也没有任何紫外光源。家庭小实验用的激光笔与仪器无关，有它自己的安全说明。
