1 · BLIND ≠ INDEPENDENT1 · 盲测 ≠ 独立

A red team built a gel we never saw"红队"造了一块我们没见过的凝胶

The old blind gel came from the same generator, and the pipeline was handed the before/after pairing. Now a separate AI agent (another Claude instance, so it may share our blind spots), which read only the data format and never the detector code, built gel S3: lost fibrils, debris, a fold, a bubble, curved and bundled fibrils, tilted fibrils, expansion drifting during the scan, and its own blur and noise. The pipeline now matches the fibrils itself, and its result on S3 is published as the red team scored it.

2 · THE BEFORE-IMAGE IS LOAD-BEARING2 · 膨胀前的图是承重的

A go/no-go for the stripes给条纹定一条继续/放弃线

simulated The axial ruler is only as good as the label-free stripes before expansion. Its error follows one number, q = striation contrast × √photons: go if q ≥ {{n:tested.q_go}} (1% per myofibril at 10 sarcomeres). An uneven light that the old code ignored no longer matters: a 20% slope cost {{n:tested.slope_v1}}, now {{n:tested.slope_v2}}. predicted From the physics of each mode, the choice is differential phase contrast with four oblique LEDs (predicted contrast {{n:tested.dpc_m}}, q ≈ {{n:tested.dpc_q}}). Crossed polarisers lose fibrils near 0° and 90°: only {{n:tested.pol_cover}} of orientations reach the go line.

模拟轴向标尺好不好，取决于膨胀前那张免标记图上的条纹。它的误差由一个数决定：q = 条纹反差 × √光子数。q ≥ {{n:tested.q_go}} 就继续（10 个肌节时每根肌原纤维误差 1%）。旧代码没处理的光照不均现在不碍事了：20% 的斜坡原来造成 {{n:tested.slope_v1}} 误差，现在是 {{n:tested.slope_v2}}。预测按每种成像方式的物理推算，选定四个斜射 LED 的差分相衬（预计反差 {{n:tested.dpc_m}}，q 约 {{n:tested.dpc_q}}）。正交偏振会丢掉接近 0° 和 90° 方向的纤维：只有 {{n:tested.pol_cover}} 的方向能达到继续线。

3 · A −0.1% BIAS3 · −0.1% 的偏差

Broken ends, and effect sizes instead of "significant"断头的问题，以及用效应大小代替"显著"

simulated The ladder used the Z-discs at the very ends of each fragment, which are cut in half, and that pulled the axial factor low. They are now ignored. The gel-mean error on our own five development gels is now {{n:tested.v2_mean_err}}, and the per-myofibril error {{n:tested.v2_rms}}. "The confidence interval excludes zero" had called a perfect gel anisotropic; now anisotropy counts only beyond ±1%. On the perfect gel the estimate is {{n:tested.v2_s0_aniso}}, called {{n:tested.v2_s0_call}}; a planted 2% anisotropy reads {{n:tested.v2_s2_aniso}} (truth {{n:tested.v2_s2_truth}}).

模拟"梯子"用了每段碎片最两端的 Z 盘，而那两个 Z 盘只剩半个，把轴向倍数拉低了。现在端头的 Z 盘不再使用。我们自己的五块开发用合成凝胶，整体平均误差现在是 {{n:tested.v2_mean_err}}，每根肌原纤维的误差是 {{n:tested.v2_rms}}。原来"置信区间不包含零"会把一块完美的凝胶判成各向异性；现在只有超过 ±1% 才算。在完美凝胶上，估计值是 {{n:tested.v2_s0_aniso}}，判为{{n:tested.v2_s0_call_zh}}；人为埋入的 2% 各向异性测得 {{n:tested.v2_s2_aniso}}（真值 {{n:tested.v2_s2_truth}}）。

After the gel sets, and the gap measured在凝胶凝固之后拍，并测量中间这段

untested A protocol decision, never run. The protocol took the before-image before anchoring and gelling, so any change in sarcomere length in between would read as an expansion error. The reference image is now taken after the gel sets, on the rinsed gel, and the first three gels are imaged before anchoring too, to measure what that step does.

未测试这是流程上的决定，从没实际做过。原来的流程在锚定和成胶之前拍膨胀前的图，中间肌节长度的任何变化都会被当成膨胀误差。现在参考图改在凝胶凝固、冲洗之后拍；前三块凝胶还会在锚定前再拍一次，测量这一步到底带来多少变化。

6 · THE GEL KEEPS MOVING6 · 凝胶还在动

A clock tile for scan-time drift用"时钟视野"纠正扫描时的漂移

simulated A gel can keep swelling during a two-hour scan, which would paint a fake gradient in the order the tiles were taken. One tile is now re-imaged every 50 tiles as a clock. On a synthetic gel drifting by 0.5%, the error trend falls from {{n:tested.drift_slope_none}} to {{n:tested.drift_slope_clock}}.

模拟两个小时的扫描里，凝胶可能还在继续膨胀，结果会按拍摄顺序画出一个假的梯度。现在每拍 50 个视野就回去重拍同一个"时钟视野"。在一块漂移 0.5% 的合成凝胶上，误差随时间的趋势从 {{n:tested.drift_slope_none}} 降到 {{n:tested.drift_slope_clock}}。
