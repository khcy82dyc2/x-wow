Why this exists为什么做这个

The credit goes to whoever builds it.功劳归动手的人。

X-WOW's founder isn't interested in money, fame or research credit. These are ideas X-WOW thinks are great, and the founder would rather see them built than see a name on them.

X-WOW 的创办人对钱、名气和科研署名都没兴趣。这些是 X-WOW 觉得很棒的点子；比起署上谁的名字，创办人更想看到它们真的被造出来。

If you build one, it's yours. Improve it, publish it, teach with it, and you're welcome to claim the success. CC0 lets you sell it too, but it can't clear anyone else's patents or the safety rules for selling a device: each invention has a legal note.

你造出来，它就是你的。改进它、发表它、拿去教学都行，成功尽管算你的。专利允许的地方，拿去卖也可以；每个发明都附有法律说明。

Mission使命

X-WOW began as the Pathology Without Borders Initiative. Now it widens to borderless invention: ways to see more closely and to make the tools, with no gatekeepers, given away free.

X-WOW 起步于“无国界病理”计划。现在它扩展为无国界发明：看得更近的办法和做工具的办法，没有门槛，免费送出。
