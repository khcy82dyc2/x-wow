Why this exists为什么做这个

Take it. Build it. Claim it.拿走，造出来，算你的。

I'm not interested in money, fame or research credit. These are ideas I think are great, and I'd rather see them built than see my name on them.

我对钱、名气和科研署名都没兴趣。这些是我觉得很棒的点子，比起署上我的名字，我更想看到它们真的被造出来。

So I make each one as complete and as convenient to build as I can: the design, a priced parts list, the maths, the safety rules, the simulations, and an honest list of what is still unproven. Then I give it away.

所以每一个我都尽量做完整、做方便：设计、带价格的零件清单、算式、安全规则、模拟结果，还有一份老老实实的“还没证明”清单。然后免费送出去。

If you build one, it's yours. Improve it, publish it, teach with it, and you're welcome to claim the success. Selling it is fine too where the patents allow; each invention has a legal note.

你造出来，它就是你的。改进它、发表它、拿去教学都行，成功尽管算你的。专利允许的地方，拿去卖也可以；每个发明都附有法律说明。

If you like, tell me how it went, so the next person has an easier time.

愿意的话，告诉我结果，让下一个人走得轻松一点。

Mission使命

X-WOW began as the Pathology Without Borders Initiative. Now it widens to borderless invention: lab-grade seeing and making, with no gatekeepers, given away free.

X-WOW 起步于“无国界病理”计划。现在它扩展为无国界发明：实验室级的观察与制造，没有门槛，免费送出。

Complete完整

Design, parts, prices, maths, safety and what's unproven. Nothing held back.

设计、零件、价格、算式、安全、还没证明的地方，一样不藏。

Honest老实

Every number has a source. Simulated is labelled simulated. No claim goes beyond what the checks show.

每个数字都有出处；模拟的就标“模拟”；不说超出检验结果的话。

Free免费

CC0 for text, data and code; CERN-OHL-P for hardware. No sign-up, no paywall, no tracking.

文字、数据、代码用 CC0；硬件用 CERN-OHL-P。不用注册，没有付费墙，不追踪你。
