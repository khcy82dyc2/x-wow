What came before之前做过的事

From 2018 to 2020, X-WOW ran lectures, a kids' science club and free teaching material, and shared digital slides and a slide scanner. These pages keep the record and credit the people who made it happen.

2018 到 2020 年间，X-WOW 办过讲座、儿童科学俱乐部，做过免费教材，也分享过数字切片和切片扫描仪。这些页面留下记录，也感谢让这一切发生的人。
