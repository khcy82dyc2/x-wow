X-WOW Kids Science ClubX-WOW 儿童科学俱乐部

University students in Abuja started a science club around a microscope, books and slides that X-WOW sent, to show schoolchildren the microbial world. X-WOW funded it. The students ran it.

阿布贾的大学生用 X-WOW 寄去的显微镜、书和切片办起一个科学俱乐部，想让孩子们看看微生物的世界。X-WOW 出钱，学生们出力。
