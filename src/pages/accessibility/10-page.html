Accessibility

Everyone should be able to use X-WOW

We aim for the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. The site has been checked with automated screenshots, not yet audited by a person, so we don't claim it conforms.

What the site supports

  • Headings, landmarks and lists marked up properly, so screen readers can move around a page.
  • Links, buttons and controls are built to be reached with a keyboard and show a visible focus ring; this hasn't been tested page by page.
  • If your device asks for reduced motion, the opening animation is skipped, the 3D models and the duck in the margin stay still.
  • SWELL's 3D model turns slowly on its own, with a moving scan ring and light path. The Pause button under it stops all of that and Play starts it again (also on a device that asks for reduced motion, where it starts paused); your choice lasts until you close the tab.
  • Pictures carry text alternatives; drawings, renders and AI-made images say on the image what they are.
  • Text colours were chosen for contrast on the dark background and looked at in screenshots, not measured with a contrast tool; pages work on phones and slow connections, and the slide game's pictures load only when needed (the SWELL page's 3D model loads with the page).
  • The read-aloud button uses only a voice that runs on your own device.

Known gaps

  • The 3D concept model is visual and turns only with a mouse, finger or trackpad, not with keys. A labelled drawing of the same parts shows before it loads, and each part's description is text, but the model itself can't be explored with a screen reader.
  • SHRINK, the slide game, is a visual zoom game. Its facts and field guide are text, but finding a spot on a slide needs sight.
  • The site is in English only for now; Chinese versions are hidden until they can be kept up to date.

Tell us

If something is hard or impossible to use, write to info@x-wow.com and say which page and what happened.
