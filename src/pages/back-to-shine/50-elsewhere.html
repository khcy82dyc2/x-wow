LabAid (UK)（英国） Schools in developing countries; in 2024/25, schools and colleges in Cameroon, Ghana, Liberia, Nigeria, Sierra Leone, The Gambia, Uganda and Zimbabwe发展中国家的学校；2024/25 年度供给了喀麦隆、加纳、利比里亚、尼日利亚、塞拉利昂、冈比亚、乌干达和津巴布韦的中小学与学院 Volunteers check and refurbish surplus science equipment from UK schools, microscopes included, and give it free to schools that request it.志愿者检查、翻新英国学校淘汰的科学器材（包括显微镜），免费送给提出申请的学校。
Seeding Labs (USA)（美国） University scientists in under-resourced settings worldwide全球资源匮乏地区的大学科研人员 Its Instrumental Access programme ships donated research equipment and supplies to labs abroad.通过“Instrumental Access”项目，把捐赠的科研仪器和耗材运到海外实验室。
EquipSent (Zurich)（苏黎世） Schools and labs that can't afford equipment; projects listed in Nigeria, South Africa, Cameroon, Malawi, Kenya, Chile, Mexico, Pakistan and elsewhere买不起设备的学校和实验室；项目列表包括尼日利亚、南非、喀麦隆、马拉维、肯尼亚、智利、墨西哥、巴基斯坦等地 A non-profit association that collects unused lab equipment and transfers it to the schools and labs that apply.一家非营利协会，收集闲置的实验设备，转给提出申请的学校和实验室。
Project C.U.R.E. (USA)（美国） Hospitals and clinics in resource-limited countries资源有限国家的医院和诊所 Volunteers pack and deliver ocean containers of donated medical equipment and supplies.志愿者把捐赠的医疗设备和物资装进海运集装箱，送到当地。
MedShare (USA)（美国） Underserved facilities worldwide and in the USA全球和美国国内缺医少药的医疗机构 Collects surplus medical products from hospitals, distributors and manufacturers and redistributes them; also repairs biomedical equipment.从医院、经销商和厂家回收富余的医疗物资再分发出去，也做生物医学设备维修。
