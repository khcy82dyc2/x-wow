The old blind gel came from the same generator, and the pipeline was handed the before/after pairing. Now a separate program, which read only the data format and never the detector code, built a new gel (it may still share some of our blind spots): lost fibrils, debris, a fold, a bubble, curved and bundled fibrils, tilted fibrils, expansion drifting during the scan, and its own blur and noise. simulated What failed: the transverse ruler read widths about a fifth too small on average, which made the anisotropy call wrong in size (the sign was right). The cause is not the blur calibration: rerun with the true blur widths, the bias is still {{n:tested.s3_true_psf_bias}}. It is a blur shape the ruler does not model, and the pipeline's own landmark ruler already disagreed with it by {{n:tested.s3r_tr_vs_land}} while the call was still made. Fixed afterwards (a development fix, since this gel has now been seen): no anisotropy call while the two rulers disagree by more than 3%. On this gel the call is now {{n:tested.s3r_aniso_call}}. The ordinary under- and over-expanded patches, the curved fibrils and the tilted region went unflagged, and only about a quarter of the pairs got an axial ratio. The pairing, the debris rejection and the gel-level factor held. Like our own gels, this gel reached the pipeline as one set of profiles per object, cut out of its builder's images by an emulated image front-end, so SWELL's own code never segmented or stitched an image in these tests: read the pairing and debris scores as best cases for real images. The drift correction held too, but the slope in the table can't show it: the planted drift rose to {{n:tested.s3_drift_planted}} and fell back, so even with no correction its straight-line slope would have been only {{n:tested.s3_drift_uncorr}}, smaller than that slope's standard error. What shows it is that after the clock tile, the median error in each of six time bins stays within ±{{n:tested.s3_drift_bins}}. The slope also leaves out {{n:tested.s3_drift_excluded}} of its {{n:tested.s3_drift_n}} myofibrils, those with errors beyond 5%; with them it reads {{n:tested.s3_drift_all}}. The local expansion flag already uses the landmark ruler's area change as well as the axial ratio, but it fires only where the change, smoothed by a Gaussian of {{n:tested.flag_kernel}} standard deviation, exceeds {{n:tested.flag_floor}}, or four standard errors where that is larger, and nothing in the under-expanded patch crossed that line. How a patch's size and strength decide whether it is caught has not been measured. These are open problems; this gel has now been seen, so each fix needs a new blind gel.

模拟没过的地方：横向标尺把每根纤维的宽度都量小了约五分之一，于是各向异性的大小判断错了（方向是对的）。原因不在模糊校准：给它真实的模糊宽度再跑，偏差仍有 {{n:tested.s3_true_psf_bias}}。原因是一种它没建模的模糊形状；流水线自己的地标标尺当时已经和它相差 {{n:tested.s3r_tr_vs_land}}，可结论照样下了。S3 之后的修正（属于开发阶段的修正，因为 S3 已经"看过"）：两把标尺相差超过 3% 时，不下各向异性的结论。现在在 S3 上的结论是{{n:tested.s3r_aniso_call_zh}}。普通的膨胀不足和过度区域、弯曲的纤维和倾斜的区域都没被标出来，而且只有大约四分之一的配对得到了轴向比值。配对、排除碎屑和整块凝胶的倍数扛住了。和我们自己的凝胶一样，S3 交给流水线的是每个物体各一组轮廓，由红队的模拟图像前端从它的图像里切出来，所以 SWELL 自己的代码从没分割或拼接过一张图像：配对和排除碎屑的成绩，要看作真实图像下的最好情况。漂移校正也扛住了，但表里的斜率显示不出这一点：埋入的漂移先升到 {{n:tested.s3_drift_planted}} 再回落，所以即使完全不校正，它的直线斜率也只有 {{n:tested.s3_drift_uncorr}}，比这个斜率的标准误还小。能说明校正有效的是：经时钟视野校正后，六个时间段各自的中位误差都在 ±{{n:tested.s3_drift_bins}} 以内。这个斜率还剔除了 {{n:tested.s3_drift_n}} 根肌原纤维中误差超过 5% 的 {{n:tested.s3_drift_excluded}} 根；算上它们，斜率是 {{n:tested.s3_drift_all}}。局部膨胀的标记本来就同时看地标标尺的面积变化和轴向比值，但只有在经标准差 {{n:tested.flag_kernel}} 的高斯平滑后，变化超过 {{n:tested.flag_floor}}（或者四倍标准误，取较大者）的地方才会触发；膨胀不足的区域里没有一处越过这条线。一个区域的大小和强弱怎样决定它能不能被抓到，还没有测过。这些都列为未解问题；S3 现在已经"看过"了，每一次修正都需要一块新的盲测凝胶。

2 · THE BEFORE-IMAGE IS LOAD-BEARING2 · 膨胀前的图是承重的

A go/no-go for the stripes给条纹定一条继续/放弃线

simulated The axial ruler is only as good as the label-free stripes before expansion. Its error follows one number, q = striation contrast × √photons: go if q ≥ {{n:tested.q_go}} (1% per myofibril at 10 sarcomeres). An uneven light that the old code ignored no longer seems to matter: with a 20% slope the per-myofibril error was {{n:tested.slope_v1}} with the old code and is {{n:tested.slope_v2}} now, against {{n:tested.slope0_v1}} and {{n:tested.slope0_v2}} with even light. Each figure is the RMS of its own {{n:tested.slope_trials}} random draws, not shared between cases, with no confidence interval, so small differences mean little: the new code's lower figure with a slope than without one should not be read as a gain. predicted From the physics of each mode, the choice is differential phase contrast with four oblique LEDs (predicted contrast {{n:tested.dpc_m}}, q ≈ {{n:tested.dpc_q}}, assuming about {{n:tested.dpc_n}} detected photons per sample and shot noise only). In one acquisition, crossed polarisers lose fibrils near 0° and 90°: only {{n:tested.pol_cover}} of orientations reach the go line. A second acquisition with the polariser pair turned 45° was not simulated.

模拟轴向标尺好不好，取决于膨胀前那张免标记图上的条纹。它的误差由一个数决定：q = 条纹反差 × √光子数。q ≥ {{n:tested.q_go}} 就继续（10 个肌节时每根肌原纤维误差 1%）。旧代码没处理的光照不均现在看来不碍事了：有 20% 的斜坡时，每根肌原纤维的误差在旧代码下是 {{n:tested.slope_v1}}，现在是 {{n:tested.slope_v2}}；光照均匀时分别是 {{n:tested.slope0_v1}} 和 {{n:tested.slope0_v2}}。每个数都是各自 {{n:tested.slope_trials}} 次随机抽样的均方根，各情形之间不共用抽样，也没有置信区间，所以小的差别说明不了什么：新代码有斜坡时反而比没斜坡时低，不能当成改进。预测按每种成像方式的物理推算，选定四个斜射 LED 的差分相衬（预计反差 {{n:tested.dpc_m}}，q 约 {{n:tested.dpc_q}}，前提是每个采样点约 {{n:tested.dpc_n}} 个被探测到的光子，且只有散粒噪声）。只拍一次时，正交偏振会丢掉接近 0° 和 90° 方向的纤维：只有 {{n:tested.pol_cover}} 的方向能达到继续线。把偏振片对转 45° 再拍一次的做法没有模拟过。

3 · A −0.1% BIAS3 · −0.1% 的偏差

Broken ends, and effect sizes instead of "significant"断头的问题，以及用效应大小代替"显著"

simulated The ladder used the Z-discs at the very ends of each fragment, which are cut in half, and that pulled the axial factor low. They are now ignored. The gel-mean error on our own five development gels is now {{n:tested.v2_mean_err}}, and the per-myofibril error {{n:tested.v2_rms}} (under the simulated conditions listed in the numbers). "The confidence interval excludes zero" had called a perfect gel anisotropic; now anisotropy counts only beyond ±1%. On the perfect gel the estimate is {{n:tested.v2_s0_aniso}}, called {{n:tested.v2_s0_call}}; a planted 2% anisotropy reads {{n:tested.v2_s2_aniso}} (truth {{n:tested.v2_s2_truth}}). The brackets are 95% confidence intervals from resampling myofibrils, which treats them as independent and leaves out error they all share, such as the blur and cross-section model, so they are too narrow: they miss the true value on {{n:tested.v2_ci_miss}} of our {{n:tested.v2_n_gels}} own gels, and on the separately built gel. The ±1% rule also hides a small bias: on our own gels the transverse ruler reads {{n:tested.v2_etr_bias}} high. A corrected interval (the bias removed, a term for shared error, its coverage checked by simulation, whole gels as the unit) is an open problem.

模拟"梯子"用了每段碎片最两端的 Z 盘，而那两个 Z 盘只剩半个，把轴向倍数拉低了。现在端头的 Z 盘不再使用。我们自己的五块开发用合成凝胶，整体平均误差现在是 {{n:tested.v2_mean_err}}，每根肌原纤维的误差是 {{n:tested.v2_rms}}（在数字一节列出的模拟条件下）。原来"置信区间不包含零"会把一块完美的凝胶判成各向异性；现在只有超过 ±1% 才算。在完美凝胶上，估计值是 {{n:tested.v2_s0_aniso}}，判为{{n:tested.v2_s0_call_zh}}；人为埋入的 2% 各向异性测得 {{n:tested.v2_s2_aniso}}（真值 {{n:tested.v2_s2_truth}}）。方括号里是对肌原纤维重抽样得到的 95% 置信区间：这种算法把每根纤维当成彼此独立，漏掉了它们共有的误差（比如模糊和横截面模型），所以区间太窄：在我们自己的 {{n:tested.v2_n_gels}} 块凝胶里有 {{n:tested.v2_ci_miss}} 块没覆盖真值，在 S3 上也没覆盖。±1% 的规则还掩盖了一个小偏差：在我们自己的凝胶上，横向标尺读高了 {{n:tested.v2_etr_bias}}。修正后的区间（去掉这个偏差、加上共有误差项、用模拟检查覆盖率、以整块凝胶为单位）仍是未解问题。

4 · WHEN THE BEFORE-IMAGE IS TAKEN4 · 膨胀前的图什么时候拍

After the gel sets, and the gap measured在凝胶凝固之后拍，并测量中间这段

untested A protocol decision, never run. The protocol took the before-image before anchoring and gelling, so any change in sarcomere length in between would read as an expansion error. The reference image is now taken after the gel sets, on the rinsed gel, and the first three gels are imaged before anchoring too, to measure what that step does. The rinse is not settled either: the plan rinses the gel in PBS, a salt solution, and a sodium-acrylate gel may start to swell or buckle in it (reasoning, not a measurement), so the first gels must also show that the rinse leaves the gel's size unchanged, or the rinse must move to a liquid that does.

未测试这是流程上的决定，从没实际做过。原来的流程在锚定和成胶之前拍膨胀前的图，中间肌节长度的任何变化都会被当成膨胀误差。现在参考图改在凝胶凝固、冲洗之后拍；前三块凝胶还会在锚定前再拍一次，测量这一步到底带来多少变化。冲洗这一步也还没定：方案用 PBS 冲洗凝胶，PBS 是一种盐溶液，丙烯酸钠凝胶在里面可能已经开始膨胀或起皱（这是一次 AI 审查的推理，不是测量），所以前几块凝胶还必须证明冲洗不改变凝胶的大小，否则冲洗就要换成不改变它大小的液体。

5 · ENOUGH LIGHT5 · 光够不够

How much dye the signal needs信号需要多少染料

For a signal-to-noise ratio of 10 at today's exposure, {{n:tested.lab_min_100}} of the protein's amines must carry a dye ({{n:tested.lab_min_200}} at 200 ms). A pan-ExM-strength stain is estimated to label about {{n:tested.lab_pan}} (range {{n:tested.lab_pan_range}}); the original recipe's weaker stain, about {{n:tested.lab_kit}} (range {{n:tested.lab_kit_range}}), probably falls short: only the top of its range reaches what is needed. The model treats every amine in the protein as free to take a dye, but the anchoring step is expected to use some amines first, which it leaves out, so the real share could be lower. So the first gels titrate stronger stains. untested Still open until real gels measure it.

要在现在的曝光下达到信噪比 10，蛋白质上 {{n:tested.lab_min_100}} 的氨基需要带上染料（200 毫秒曝光时是 {{n:tested.lab_min_200}}）。按 pan-ExM 的染色浓度，估计能标记约 {{n:tested.lab_pan}}（范围 {{n:tested.lab_pan_range}}）；原配方较淡的染色约 {{n:tested.lab_kit}}（范围 {{n:tested.lab_kit_range}}），很可能不够：只有范围的上端够得着需要的量。这个模型把蛋白质上的每个氨基都当作能接上染料，但锚定那一步预计会先用掉一部分氨基，模型没算这一点，所以实际比例可能更低。所以第一批凝胶要试更浓的染色。未测试在真实凝胶测出来之前，这仍是未解问题。

6 · THE GEL KEEPS MOVING6 · 凝胶还在动

A clock tile for scan-time drift用"时钟视野"纠正扫描时的漂移

simulated A gel can keep swelling during a two-hour scan, which would paint a fake gradient in the order the tiles were taken. One tile is now re-imaged every 50 tiles as a clock. On a synthetic gel drifting by 0.5%, the error trend falls from {{n:tested.drift_slope_none}} to {{n:tested.drift_slope_clock}} (straight-line slopes, without standard errors: none were computed for this gel).

模拟两个小时的扫描里，凝胶可能还在继续膨胀，结果会按拍摄顺序画出一个假的梯度。现在每拍 50 个视野就回去重拍同一个"时钟视野"。在一块漂移 0.5% 的合成凝胶上，误差随时间的趋势从 {{n:tested.drift_slope_none}} 降到 {{n:tested.drift_slope_clock}}（都是直线斜率，没有标准误：这块凝胶没算标准误）。

Still failing: on the blind gel, the gently stretched patch is flagged mostly by the wrong detector ({{n:tested.v2_blind_stretch_own}} by its own, {{n:tested.v2_blind_stretch_any}} by any), and a 1.5 µm tear stays below the detection limit. Both are listed as open problems.

仍未通过：在盲测凝胶上，那块被轻轻拉伸的区域主要被"错的"检测器标出来（自己的检测器只标出 {{n:tested.v2_blind_stretch_own}}，任何检测器合计 {{n:tested.v2_blind_stretch_any}}），1.5 µm 的裂缝仍低于检测下限。两条都列为未解问题。
