Science为什么行得通

The three ideas behind SWELL让它保持便宜的三个点子

The project's design rule: chemistry does the resolution work, so the optics can stay modest. More expansion with a modest lens is meant to stand in for expensive glass.

项目的设计原则：分辨率交给化学，光学就可以保持普通。多胀几倍、配一个普通镜头，是想用来替代昂贵的玻璃。

1 · Expand the sample instead of buying better lenses1 · 放大样本，而不是升级镜头

A lens cannot separate two points closer than about 0.61·λ/NA. For the {{n:swell.na_a}} NA objective at {{n:swell.lam_em}} that is {{n:swell.res_lens_a}}. Expansion moves the molecules apart before the light ever reaches the lens, and the project's rule of thumb is effective resolution ≈ optical resolution ÷ E. In original-specimen units that is about {{n:swell.res_exp_a}} at {{n:swell.res_e_a}}, and {{n:swell.res_exp_b}} at {{n:swell.res_e_b}} with the {{n:swell.na_b}} NA objective (optics alone: that track's camera samples at {{n:swell.nyq_b}} Nyquist, a little coarser than the lens resolves, so detail near this limit can alias).

镜头分不开比约 0.61·λ/NA 更近的两个点。数值孔径 {{n:swell.na_a}} 的物镜在 {{n:swell.lam_em}} 波长下，这个距离是 {{n:swell.res_lens_a}}。膨胀在光到达镜头之前就把分子拉开了；项目的经验公式是：有效分辨率 ≈ 光学分辨率 ÷ E。换算回原样本尺度，{{n:swell.res_e_a}} 时约 {{n:swell.res_exp_a}}；换数值孔径 {{n:swell.na_b}} 的物镜、放大 {{n:swell.res_e_b}} 时约 {{n:swell.res_exp_b}}（只算光学：这条路线的相机采样是奈奎斯特的 {{n:swell.nyq_b}}，比镜头能分辨的略粗，接近这个极限的细节可能出现混叠）。

The lens's diffraction limit does not change; the specimen gets bigger. These are paper numbers, and still not electron microscopy: you only see what you label, and the chemistry can make its own artefacts.

Who got there first: Zhang, Chang and colleagues paired expansion with a webcam mini-microscope in 2016 ("expansion mini-microscopy"), reaching, after expansion, a resolution comparable to a benchtop microscope's on unexpanded samples, at a hundredth to a thousandth of the price. SWELL builds on their idea.

谁先做到的：Zhang、Chang 等人在 2016 年就把膨胀显微和网络摄像头做的迷你显微镜结合起来（"膨胀迷你显微"），膨胀之后，用百分之一到千分之一的价格，达到了与台式显微镜看未膨胀样品相当的分辨率。SWELL 是在他们的思路上往前走。

镜头的衍射极限一点没变，变大的是样本。这些都是纸面计算，也远不是电镜：只能看到被标记的东西，化学步骤本身还可能制造假象。

2 · The muscle's stripes as a built-in ruler2 · 长在肉里的尺子

E = SLpost ÷ SLpre

Sarcomere spacing is measured on the same myofibril before and after expansion. The project's notes call the period blur-invariant: blur softens each stripe but leaves the spacing alone, so the ratio survives modest optics. The before-image should need no label: differential phase contrast with four oblique LEDs is predicted to show the stripes (the project's choice after the physics check; untested), with crossed polarisers as a cross-check. That method builds on asymmetric-illumination differential phase contrast as Mehta and Sheppard described it (2009), and on the quantitative, multi-angle form Tian and Waller showed on an LED array microscope (2015).

在同一根肌原纤维上，膨胀前后各量一次肌节间距。项目笔记说这个周期“不受模糊影响”：模糊只让条纹变软，间距不变，所以用普通的光学件，这个比值也不受影响。膨胀前那张图应该不用染色：按预测，用四个斜射 LED 做差分相衬（DPC）能看到条纹（这是物理核算后项目选定的方式，未经测试），正交偏振光作为交叉核对。这种方法建立在 Mehta 与 Sheppard 描述的非对称照明差分相衬（2009）之上，也建立在 Tian 与 Waller 在 LED 阵列显微镜上展示的定量、多角度形式（2015）之上。

It replaced the textbook A-band ruler, which needs sarcomeres long enough to show an I-band: only about {{n:swell.aband_usable_a}} of supermarket breast's sarcomeres at {{n:swell.track_a}}, computed for a mean length of {{n:swell.sl_breast}} with an assumed, not measured, spread of {{n:swell.sl_breast_sd}} (standard deviation).

它取代了教科书里的 A 带尺子。那把尺子要求肌节长到能看见 I 带：按平均长度 {{n:swell.sl_breast}}、假设（不是测量）的离散 {{n:swell.sl_breast_sd}}（标准差）计算，超市鸡胸肉的肌节在 {{n:swell.track_a}} 下只有约 {{n:swell.aband_usable_a}} 合格。

Who got there first: Chen, Tillberg and Boyden (2015), who introduced expansion microscopy, imaged the same cells before and after expansion and measured how far lengths moved (under 1% of the distance in cultured cells, 2–4% in brain slices). Sheard and colleagues (2019) used the periodicity of sarcomeres and the fixed width of Z-discs as intrinsic reporters of local gel expansion in heart cells, and Sheard's 2021 thesis found the sarcomere factor lower than the gel's. A 2022 thesis by Migliore expanded isolated rabbit myofibrils, imaged them without labels and used the A-band as the ruler. VISTA (Miao, Lin, Qian and Wei, 2022) measured expansion by comparing label-free images of the same structures before and after. In our dated searches (docs/design/prior_art.md in the download, 10 October 2026) we found nothing that pairs each myofibril's label-free period before expansion with its own period after.

谁先做到的：发明膨胀显微术的 Chen、Tillberg 与 Boyden（2015）就在膨胀前后拍同一批细胞，量出长度偏移了多少（培养细胞里不到距离的 1%，脑切片里 2–4%）。Sheard 等人（2019）在心肌细胞里，用肌节的周期和 Z 盘的固定宽度作为凝胶局部膨胀的内在指示；Sheard 2021 年的学位论文发现，肌节算出的倍数比凝胶的低。Migliore 2022 年的学位论文膨胀过分离的兔肌原纤维，不染色成像，并用 A 带当尺子。VISTA（Miao、Lin、Qian 与 Wei，2022）通过比较同一结构膨胀前后的免标记图像来测膨胀。在我们有日期的检索里（下载包中的 docs/design/prior_art.md，2026 年 10 月 10 日），没有找到把每一根肌原纤维膨胀前的免标记周期和它自己膨胀后的周期配对的做法。

The stock OpenFlexure lens would undersample ({{n:swell.ofm_stock_nyq}} Nyquist), and a colour sensor wastes signal, so neither is used. The stage would be a fork of the open OpenFlexure Microscope v7, run by a Raspberry Pi 5 and a Pico 2.

OpenFlexure 自带的镜头会欠采样（只有奈奎斯特的 {{n:swell.ofm_stock_nyq}}），彩色传感器又浪费信号，所以都不用。载物台将改自开源的 OpenFlexure 显微镜 v7，由树莓派 5 和 Pico 2 控制。
