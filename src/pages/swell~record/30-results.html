The first desk calculation said one myofibril segment of {{n:swell.first_est_n}} sarcomeres gives its expansion to {{n:swell.first_est}} (RMS), for assumed before-image contrast of {{n:swell.contrast_range}}. It assumed perfectly regular sarcomeres. Real ones vary by about {{n:swell.jitter}} from one to the next, and under that jitter (and at some sarcomere lengths) the original estimator (a free-frequency fit) locked onto side-lobes: {{n:swell.sidelobe_frac}} of myofibrils came out wrong by {{n:swell.sidelobe_err}}. 第一次纸面计算说：一段含 {{n:swell.first_est_n}} 个肌节的肌原纤维，放大倍数能测准到 {{n:swell.first_est}}（均方根误差），前提是膨胀前图像的对比度在 {{n:swell.contrast_range}} 之间。可这个计算假设肌节长得完全一样。真实肌节一节和一节之间差约 {{n:swell.jitter}}，原来的估计方法（自由频率拟合）一遇到这种抖动（以及某些肌节长度）就会锁到旁瓣上：{{n:swell.sidelobe_frac}} 的肌原纤维被算错 {{n:swell.sidelobe_err}}。第一次整块凝胶模拟因此挂掉了 {{n:swell.first_run_fail}} 项检查。

The fix is the Z-disc ladder: the Z-discs in the expanded image define a sarcomere-by-sarcomere coordinate, and the before-image is fitted on that ladder, so the jitter is carried along instead of fought. Segments need at least {{n:swell.min_sarc}} sarcomeres. Result: {{n:swell.ladder_d33}} RMS per myofibril across simulation runs, {{n:swell.ladder_final}} across the four gels of the final run and {{n:swell.ladder_blind}} on the blind gel, with at most {{n:swell.ladder_outliers_final}} of myofibrils off by more than {{n:swell.outlier_band}}.

修正办法叫 Z 盘梯子：用膨胀后图像里的 Z 盘标出一节一节的肌节坐标，再沿着这把“梯子”去拟合膨胀前的图像，抖动被顺着带走，而不是硬扛。每段至少要 {{n:swell.min_sarc}} 个肌节。结果：各轮模拟中每根肌原纤维的误差为 {{n:swell.ladder_d33}}（均方根），最终一轮四块凝胶为 {{n:swell.ladder_final}}，盲测凝胶为 {{n:swell.ladder_blind}}；偏差超过 {{n:swell.outlier_band}} 的肌原纤维最多占 {{n:swell.ladder_outliers_final}}。

These precision figures hold only under the simulation's conditions: before-image stripes with a fundamental contrast of {{n:tested.sim_m_fund}} at {{n:tested.sim_photons_pre}} detected photons per sample (q ≈ {{n:tested.sim_q}}), shot noise only, a Gaussian blur of exactly known width, and one-dimensional profiles handed straight to the ruler, with no image to segment or stitch. Transverse isotropy and the shape-aware ruler横向各向同性与“认形状”的尺子

A gel can swell evenly along a myofibril and unevenly across it. So the pass criteria include transverse isotropy, Eax ÷ Etr − 1 within {{n:swell.tr_gate}} (95% CI, n ≥ {{n:swell.tr_n}} myofibrils), and the axial and transverse factors are always reported separately. That interval counts only the scatter between myofibrils, not error they all share, and on our synthetic gels it has proved too narrow (Tested harder, point 3), so a real test would have to use whole gels as the unit; that test isn't designed yet. On the blind gel the transverse factor per myofibril is {{n:swell.etr_blind}} (bias ± SD).

凝胶可能顺着肌原纤维胀得很匀，横着却不匀。所以合格标准里加了横向各向同性：Eax ÷ Etr − 1 落在 {{n:swell.tr_gate}} 以内（95% 置信区间，n ≥ {{n:swell.tr_n}} 根肌原纤维），轴向和横向的放大倍数永远分开报告。这个区间只算肌原纤维之间的离散，不算它们共有的误差，而在我们的合成凝胶上已经证明它太窄（见更严的考试第 3 条），所以真正的检验得以整块凝胶为单位；这种检验还没设计出来。盲测凝胶上，每根肌原纤维的横向放大倍数偏差为 {{n:swell.etr_blind}}（偏差 ± 标准差）。

The width ruler is the fragile one: it needs a model of each cross-section. A boxy cross-section fitted as an ellipse is off by {{n:swell.tr_boxy_dpc}} to {{n:swell.tr_boxy_pol}}, depending on the before-image method. So the pipeline measures each myofibril's cross-section shape from the expanded z-stack, and the bias drops to {{n:swell.tr_shape_post}}.

宽度尺子最娇气：它得知道每根纤维的横截面长什么样。把方形截面当椭圆来拟合，会差 {{n:swell.tr_boxy_dpc}} 到 {{n:swell.tr_boxy_pol}}（取决于膨胀前图像的拍法）。所以流程改成从膨胀后的 z 叠层里直接量每根纤维的截面形状，偏差降到 {{n:swell.tr_shape_post}}。

Blur calibration still matters. If the before-image blur is calibrated {{n:swell.psf5_case}} too wide, the bias is {{n:swell.tr_psf5}}, so the first gate calibrates it to {{n:swell.psf_cal}}. At that target the worst bias in our own model is {{n:swell.tr_worst_g1}}. The test gel built separately by a different AI agent showed the model is not enough: a blur shape the ruler does not model biased it by {{n:tested.s3_tr_bias}}, with the right blur widths or not.

模糊标定照样要紧。膨胀前图像的模糊如果标宽了 {{n:swell.psf5_case}}，偏差就是 {{n:swell.tr_psf5}}；所以第一道关卡要求标定到 {{n:swell.psf_cal}}。在这个精度下，按我们自己的模型最坏偏差是 {{n:swell.tr_worst_g1}}。红队凝胶 S3 证明这个模型不够：一种标尺没建模的模糊形状让它偏了 {{n:tested.s3_tr_bias}}，模糊宽度给没给对都一样。

Not enough light in the worst case最坏情况下光不够

Widefield optics see the whole thickness of an expanded myofibril, so out-of-focus light adds a background pedestal. At the lowest labelling simulated ({{n:swell.lab_worst}} of amines) on a {{n:swell.fib_worst}} myofibril, the signal-to-noise ratio falls {{n:swell.snr_worst}} against a target of {{n:swell.snr_target}}. Reaching the target needs {{n:swell.dose_worst}} the dose (exposure × irradiance), which costs bleaching. Before the pedestal was modelled, the first pass had given {{n:swell.snr_first}} across the labelling range.

宽场光学会把膨胀后肌原纤维的整个厚度都收进来，焦外的光叠成一层背景“底座”。在模拟的最低标记率（{{n:swell.lab_worst}} 的氨基被标记）、{{n:swell.fib_worst}} 粗的肌原纤维上，算上这层底座后，信噪比的变化是 {{n:swell.snr_worst}}，而目标是 {{n:swell.snr_target}}。要达标得把光剂量（曝光时间 × 光强）加到 {{n:swell.dose_worst}}，代价是荧光漂白。没算这层底座之前，第一轮估计在不同标记率下为 {{n:swell.snr_first}}。
