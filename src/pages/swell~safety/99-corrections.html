  • This table was first written from the project's notes. Each chemical's hazards are now read from the sheet cited, with HSE's GB classification of the substance beside them where it is higher: acrylamide stays "toxic" if swallowed and absorbed through the skin, and formaldehyde, once "toxic by inhalation", is fatal if inhaled as a substance. What else changed: imidazole, a reproductive toxicant in the rigor buffer, was missing and is added, with acrylic acid and sodium hydroxide for the fallback; sodium acrylate's sheet gives no irritant class, only aquatic hazards, so "irritant" is gone; "sodium azide: not used" now says the calibration beads may bring it in; water beads, once listed with an "ingestion hazard", are now out of the design, with the UK safety alert's words on why (a gut blockage that needs surgery, or choking); the light rule says which LEDs the lid interlock covers and still says never to look into the excitation LED; handling follows the sheets, with a fume hood for acrylamide, TEMED, imidazole and formaldehyde; TEMED's "strong-smelling" is gone (not a hazard class), and its "never in a domestic fridge" and raw chicken's "dedicated tools never return to a kitchen" are gone with the withdrawn plan to do any of this at home (TEMED is stored in a flammables cabinet; the chicken's tools stay dedicated).
  • 这张表最初是根据项目笔记写的。现在各化学品的危害都读自所引的 SDS，并在英国 HSE 的大不列颠清单对物质本身的分类更高时一并列出：丙烯酰胺仍是吞咽“有毒”、可经皮肤吸收；甲醛原来写“吸入有毒”，按物质本身其实是吸入致命。其他改动：僵直缓冲液里用的生殖毒性物质咪唑原来漏了，现已补上，并加上备用方案用到的丙烯酸和氢氧化钠；丙烯酸钠的 SDS 没有刺激性分类、只有水生环境危害，所以去掉了“刺激性”；“叠氮化钠：不使用”现在写明标定微球可能会把它带进来；水晶珠原来只写了“误吞危险”，现在已从设计里去掉，理由用英国安全警报的原话（可能堵塞肠道而需要手术，或导致窒息）；光的规则写明了哪些 LED 在盖子联锁范围内，并仍然要求绝不直视激发 LED；处理方式按各 SDS 调整，丙烯酰胺、TEMED、咪唑和甲醛都要在通风橱里操作；TEMED 的“气味强烈”去掉了（不是危害类别），它的“绝不放家用冰箱”和生鸡肉的“专用工具不回厨房”随着“在家做任何一步”的计划撤销而去掉（TEMED 存放在易燃品柜里，处理鸡肉的工具仍然专用）。
