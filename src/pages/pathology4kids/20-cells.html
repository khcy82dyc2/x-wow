Meet four blood cells认识四种血细胞

A drop of blood, stained and seen under a microscope, looks like a crowd. Here are four of the regulars. An AI drew them for this site, simplified on purpose: they are not photographs of real cells.

一滴血，染色后放在显微镜下看，像一大群人。下面是四位常客。这些示意图是 AI 为本站画的，故意画得简单，不是真实细胞的照片。

Red blood cell红细胞

A squashed disc with no nucleus, thinner in the middle. It is the most common cell in blood, and it carries oxygen around your body.

一个压扁的圆盘，没有细胞核，中间更薄。它是血里最多的一种细胞，负责把氧气运到全身。

Neutrophil中性粒细胞

Its nucleus is split into lobes, like a short string of beads. It is one of the first defenders when an infection starts.

细胞核分成几瓣，像一小串珠子。感染刚开始时，它是最早出动的防线之一。

Eosinophil嗜酸性粒细胞

Two round lobes like a pair of glasses, and big orange-pink dots. It responds to parasites and to allergies.

细胞核是两个圆瓣，像一副眼镜，周围是大颗的橙粉色小点。它对寄生虫和过敏都会作出反应。

Lymphocyte淋巴细胞

Small, with one big, dark, round nucleus that almost fills it. It is part of the body's immune defences.

个子小，一个深色的大圆核几乎把它塞满。它是身体免疫防御的一部分。
