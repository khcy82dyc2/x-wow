Meet four blood cells认识四种血细胞

A drop of blood, stained and magnified about 1000 times, looks like a crowd. Here are four of the regulars. Drawn for this site with AI help, simplified on purpose.

一滴血，染色后放大约 1000 倍，看起来像一大群人。下面是四位常客。图是在 AI 协助下为本站画的，故意画得简单。

Red blood cell红细胞

A squashed disc with no nucleus, thinner in the middle. One drop of blood holds millions of them, all carrying oxygen around your body.

一个压扁的圆盘，没有细胞核，中间更薄。一滴血里就有几百万个，它们把氧气运到全身。

Neutrophil中性粒细胞

Its nucleus looks like a string of three to five beads. First to arrive when bacteria get in.

细胞核像一串三到五颗的珠子。细菌一进来，它最先赶到。

Eosinophil嗜酸性粒细胞

Two round lobes like a pair of glasses, and bright pink-orange dots. It fights parasites and joins in allergies.

细胞核是两个圆瓣，像一副眼镜，周围是亮粉橙色的小点。它对付寄生虫，过敏时也会出场。

Lymphocyte淋巴细胞

Small, with one big round nucleus that almost fills it. It remembers germs it has met before.

个子小，一个大圆核几乎把它塞满。它会记住以前见过的病菌。
