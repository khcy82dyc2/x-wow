Why it exists它为什么存在

For students without a microscope给没有显微镜的学生

X-WOW wanted students who have no microscope to be able to look at real slides. In 2019 it set out to do that for a group of students in Nigeria.

X-WOW 希望没有显微镜的学生也能看到真正的切片。2019 年，它开始为尼日利亚的一群学生做这件事。

The first attempt failed. Whole-slide images were shared, but the viewer needed a far more powerful computer than the students had. About five months later, a viewer that runs in any web browser solved it: the whole collection went online, readable on any device.

第一次尝试失败了。全切片图像发过去了，可是看图软件需要的电脑比学生们手头的强太多。大约五个月后，一个在任何浏览器里都能用的看图工具解决了问题：整套切片放上网，什么设备都能看。
