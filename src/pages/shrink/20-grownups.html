What SHRINK is, and what it is not变小探险是什么，不是什么

SHRINK is a free zoom game for curious children (about 7 to 12, and anyone older) built from {{n:shrink.used}} real microscope slides: the kind of prepared teaching slides schools use, scanned at 20× so that one pixel is {{n:shrink.mpp}} across. Nothing in the pictures is drawn or generated. The {{n:shrink.postcards}} postcards in {{n:shrink.expeditions}} expeditions were placed one by one on real structures by the AI that wrote the game. Every slide's size and the screen's width are computed from pixels; the sizes in the postcard facts were estimated on the slide and rounded.

变小探险是一个给好奇孩子（大约 7 到 12 岁，大一点的也欢迎）的免费缩放游戏，用的是 {{n:shrink.used}} 张真正的显微镜玻片：就是学校里用的那种教学切片，用 20 倍扫描，每个像素只有 {{n:shrink.mpp}}。图里没有任何东西是画出来或生成出来的。{{n:shrink.expeditions}} 个探险里的 {{n:shrink.postcards}} 张明信片，都是写这个游戏的 AI 逐个放在真实结构上的。每张玻片的大小和屏幕的宽度都由像素算出；明信片知识里的尺寸是在玻片上估量后取整的。

For grown-ups: the game keeps progress only in this browser and sends nothing anywhere (privacy).给大人：游戏只把进度存在这个浏览器里，不发送到任何地方（隐私说明）。

How it plays, privacy and safety, the slides, the facts玩法、隐私和安全、玻片、事实核对

How it plays怎么玩

  • All the slides lie on one table at their true size. Zooming in is shrinking: the top bar says how wide the screen is and compares it with hairs, dust mites or red blood cells when one of them fits.
  • A postcard shows a real spot. It only counts when the child reaches the right place and the right size, so the game teaches the habit microscopists use: scan at low power, then zoom in to confirm.
  • Before the answer, children name what they found in their own words. The field guide keeps both names: theirs and the scientists'.
  • They can make postcards for each other: frame a spot, press the envelope, send the link. Good for a class treasure hunt.
  • No timers, no scores against anyone, no streaks.
  • 所有玻片都按真实大小摆在同一张桌子上。放大就是变小：顶栏会告诉你屏幕现在有多宽，合适的时候拿头发、尘螨或红细胞来比。
  • 明信片上是一个真实的地方。只有孩子既找到对的位置、又缩到对的大小才算数，这就是显微镜工作者的习惯：先用低倍找，再放大确认。
  • 看答案之前，孩子先用自己的话给找到的东西起名字。探险手册会把两个名字都记下来：他们的，和科学家的。
  • 孩子们可以互相出题：把一个地方框好，点信封，发出链接。很适合全班的寻宝游戏。
  • 没有计时，没有排名，没有连续打卡。

Privacy and safety隐私和安全

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no cookies. Progress is saved only in this browser (local storage, from the first visit) and never sent anywhere. "Start over" in the field guide empties it; clearing the site's data removes it.
  • Everything the game loads, the slide pixels included, comes from x-wow.com itself: no other server is contacted.
  • "Read it to me" uses only a voice that runs on this device; if there is none for the language, the button says so and stays silent.
  • Challenge links carry only a slide, a position and a size: nothing a child types ever goes into one.
  • The slides are animal, plant and fungus specimens. Slides of human tissue and of disease ({{n:shrink.human_disease}}) and of mammalian reproductive organs ({{n:shrink.repro}}) from the same box were left out. On 11 October 2026, {{n:shrink.withdrawn}} more organ slides (heart muscle, stomach, kidney, liver, small intestine, lung, spleen and spinal cord) were taken out too: their labels don't say which animal they came from, and the same box held human slides.
  • Two specimens are poisonous plants (castor beans, oleander); their slides say so.
  • 不用注册，没有广告，不追踪，不用 cookie。进度只保存在这个浏览器里（本地存储，第一次打开就会写入），不会发送到任何地方。探险手册里的“全部重来”会把它清空；清除这个网站的数据就会把它删掉。
  • 游戏加载的一切，包括玻片的像素，都来自 x-wow.com 本身：不联系任何别的服务器。
  • “读给我听”只用这台设备上本地运行的语音；如果没有这种语言的本地语音，按钮会说明并保持安静。
  • 出题链接里只有一张玻片、一个位置和一个大小：孩子打的字从来不会放进链接。
  • 这些玻片是动物、植物和真菌标本。同一盒里的人体组织和疾病玻片（{{n:shrink.human_disease}} 张）、哺乳动物生殖器官玻片（{{n:shrink.repro}} 张）都没有放进来。2026 年 10 月 11 日，又撤下了 {{n:shrink.withdrawn}} 张器官玻片（心肌、胃、肾、肝、小肠、肺、脾和脊髓）：它们的标签没有写是哪种动物的，而同一盒里有人体玻片。
  • 有两种标本是有毒植物（蓖麻籽、夹竹桃），对应的玻片上写明了。

Where the slides come from玻片从哪里来

A box of {{n:shrink.scans}} commercial teaching slides, scanned on an Aperio slide scanner. The web copies ({{n:shrink.web_mb}}) were white-balanced so empty glass reads white, cut into 512-pixel WebP tiles and packed into one PMTiles file per slide. The original scans of the {{n:shrink.used}} slides used ({{n:shrink.orig_gb}}) are kept with the slide-label photos and the scanner's ID fields removed and every image tile byte-identical; the owner scanned them, not as part of any job, and chose CC0 1.0 for them on 11 October 2026; they wait in an unpublished Zenodo draft until the owner publishes it. One printed label in the box was wrong (a lily anther labelled as a rotifer); the game names what the tissue shows.

一盒 {{n:shrink.scans}} 张商业教学玻片，用 Aperio 切片扫描仪扫描。网页版（{{n:shrink.web_mb}}）做了白平衡，让空玻璃显示成白色，再切成 512 像素的 WebP 小块，每张玻片打包成一个 PMTiles 文件。用到的 {{n:shrink.used}} 张玻片的原始扫描（{{n:shrink.orig_gb}}）已经保存好，去掉了玻片标签照片和扫描仪的身份字段，每一块图像数据都和原来逐字节相同；扫描是网站主人自己做的，不属于任何工作，主人在 2026 年 10 月 11 日决定用 CC0 1.0；它们放在 Zenodo 一份还没发布的草稿里，由主人来发布。盒子里有一张标签印错了（百合花药被标成了轮虫），游戏里按组织实际是什么来命名。

Checking the facts核对事实

Each postcard's fact is one or two plain sentences, written to be true and checkable; sizes come from the pixels. The facts and the game were written by an AI (Claude by Anthropic, used through Claude Code), working from the X-WOW owner's brief and orders. If you spot a mistake, a grown-up can email info@x-wow.com and it gets fixed.

每张明信片上的知识只有一两句大白话，力求准确、可以核查；尺寸都由像素算出。这些知识和游戏是 AI（Anthropic 的 Claude，通过 Claude Code）按 X-WOW 主人的要求和指令写的。发现错误，请大人写信到 info@x-wow.com，我们会改。
