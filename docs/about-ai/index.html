How X-WOW is made

This site's text and code, SWELL, the slide game SHRINK, and the little duck in the margin were made with AI (Claude, made by Anthropic), working from the owner's brief and orders. None of it has yet been checked by a person. The archive pages record X-WOW's earlier projects, run by people from 2018 to 2020.

What that means for what you read

  • On the invention pages, every number is meant to come from code or from a source that was opened and is cited, and every claim says whether it is measured, simulated, predicted or untested. The archive pages record earlier projects and don't carry those tags. If you find a number or claim that misses this, tell us.
  • Drawings, renders and AI-made images that could be taken for a photograph or a measurement say on the picture itself what they are. The cartoon duck in the margin is drawn in code by AI too.
  • None of X-WOW's physical inventions has been built; they are designs and simulations. The software (the slide game SHRINK, the swell-ruler tool) is built and runs.
