X-WOW Lecture Without BordersX-WOW 无国界讲座

During the 2020 lockdowns, experts in veterinary pathology and medicine gave free talks online, mostly over Zoom. They were aimed first at veterinary and medical students in Nigeria and across Africa, and open to anyone anywhere. Attendees could receive a certificate of participation.

2020 年封城期间，兽医病理和兽医学的专家们在网上免费开讲，大多用 Zoom。听众首先是尼日利亚和全非洲的兽医、医学学生，同时对全世界任何人开放。参加的人可以拿到一张参与证书。

11talks, June to October 2020场讲座，2020 年 6 月至 10 月
15speakers from the UK, Europe and the USA位讲者，来自英国、欧洲和美国

The online lectures were part-funded by the Royal College of Pathologists' Public Engagement Grant. Thank you.

这些线上讲座的部分经费来自英国皇家病理学家学会（RCPath）公众参与基金。谢谢。

Registrations, not unique people: one person could sign up for several talks. Summed from the old event pages, so it is a floor, not a ceiling.

这是报名人次，不是独立人数：一个人可以报好几场。数字由旧活动页加总而来，只会多不会少。

Before and after the talks讲座前后

In April 2020 the Kids Science Club ran its own four lockdown lectures on microbiology. After some talks in this series, attendees could submit a short piece of coursework; the ten best received a "Certificate of Best Coursework".

2020 年 4 月，儿童科学俱乐部自己办了四场封城期间的微生物学网课。这个系列的一些讲座之后，听众可以交一篇短作业，最好的十篇获得“最佳作业证书”。

Fifteen experts, eleven sessions, no fee to attend. Attendance cap: whatever the internet could carry.

十五位专家，十一场讲座，听课不要钱。人数上限：网络带得动多少，就是多少。
