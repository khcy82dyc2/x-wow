Online · June–October 2020 · past series

X-WOW Lecture Without Borders

During the 2020 lockdowns, experts in veterinary pathology and medicine gave free talks online, mostly over Zoom. They were aimed first at veterinary and medical students in Nigeria and across Africa, and open to anyone anywhere. Attendees could receive a certificate of participation.

11talks, June to October 2020
15speakers from the UK, Europe and the USA
14,000+registrations in total

The online lectures were part-funded by the Royal College of Pathologists' Public Engagement Grant. Thank you.

These are registrations, not unique people: one person could sign up for several talks. They are summed from the old event pages, so the real total of registrations may be higher, not lower.

Before and after the talks

In April 2020 the Kids Science Club ran its own four lockdown lectures on microbiology. After some talks in this series, attendees could submit a short piece of coursework; the ten best received a "Certificate of Best Coursework".
