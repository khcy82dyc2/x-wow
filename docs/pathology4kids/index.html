Pathology4Kids

"The simplest way to inspire the next generation of pathologists, brought to you by the real professionals." Pathologists and scientists turned what they see under the microscope into bright pictures and a few plain words a child could enjoy, and shared them under the hashtag #Pathology4Kids.

“启发下一代病理医生最简单的办法，由真正的专业人士带给你。”病理医生和科学家把显微镜下看到的东西，画成鲜艳的图，配上几句孩子听得懂的话，用 #Pathology4Kids 这个标签分享出来。

The idea was small on purpose. A pathologist spends the day looking at cells; a child has never heard the word. One picture with an arrow and a name ("these pink dots are eosinophils") closes that distance in a few seconds.

这个点子故意做得很小。病理医生每天都在看细胞，孩子却连“病理”这个词都没听过。一张图、一个箭头、一个名字（“这些粉红的点是嗜酸性粒细胞”），几秒钟就把距离拉近了。

One activity on the old site was called "draw with your microscope": a cartoon picture made under a microscope and scanned at 20× magnification, so children could zoom into it the way a pathologist zooms into tissue.

旧网站上有个活动叫“用显微镜画画”：在显微镜下做出一幅卡通画，用 20 倍放大扫描下来，孩子可以像病理医生放大组织那样，一层层放大去看。

Who made it谁做的

Contributors were pathologists and scientists in the UK and the USA, sharing on Twitter and LinkedIn. The old page pointed to the hashtag instead of listing them, so we won't guess names here.

参与者是英国和美国的病理医生与科学家，在推特和领英上分享。旧网页只给了标签，没有列名单，所以这里也不乱猜名字。

Audience: children; format: one picture, one new word. Adults were not prevented from learning.

观众：孩子；形式：一张图，一个新词。大人想跟着学，也没人拦着。

Inventions to steal, happily. Everything here is free to build, change and claim.

好发明，开心偷。这里的一切都可以免费造、随便改，成功算你的。
