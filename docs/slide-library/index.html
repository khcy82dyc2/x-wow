Why it has his name为什么用他的名字

Named after Akpan Friday以 Akpan Friday 命名

In February 2019, Akpan Friday, then a first-year microbiology student at the University of Abuja in Nigeria, explained that most students around him never get to use a real microscope. They learn microbiology from drawings.

2019 年 2 月，当时在尼日利亚阿布贾大学读微生物学一年级的 Akpan Friday 说，他身边大多数学生从来没用过真正的显微镜，只能对着图画学微生物。

The first attempt to help failed. Whole-slide images were shared, but the viewer needed a far more powerful computer than his university had, and despite trying very hard he couldn't get it to run. He kept helping anyway: he explained what students in Africa were up against, visited two primary schools to talk with teachers and wrote to the Local Education Authority.

第一次尝试失败了。全切片图像发过去了，可是看图软件需要的电脑比他学校里的强太多，他非常努力也没能跑起来。但他没有停下：他讲清楚非洲学生面对的难处，去两所小学和老师交流，还给地方教育局写信。

About five months later, a viewer that runs in any web browser solved it: the whole collection went online, readable on any device. The collection was named after Akpan, as thanks and to encourage his dream of becoming a microbiologist. He went on to found the X-WOW Kids Science Club.

大约五个月后，一个在任何浏览器里都能用的看图工具解决了问题：整套切片放上网，什么设备都能看。这套切片以 Akpan 命名，既是感谢，也鼓励他成为微生物学家的梦想。后来，他创办了 X-WOW 儿童科学俱乐部。

Where the slides live切片放在哪里

Off the old site, still online不在旧网站上，所以还在线

The scans were never stored on the old website. They sit on EasyZoom under the X-WOW account, and the old pages only framed them, which is why they survive the move to this site. The one dependency left is that account: if it ever closes, these links stop working.

这些扫描图从来没存在旧网站上。它们放在 EasyZoom 的 X-WOW 账号下，旧网页只是把它们嵌了进来，所以网站搬家后它们还在。剩下唯一的依赖就是这个账号：哪天账号关了，这些链接就失效了。

Want slides like these for your own class? A microscope with a camera and stitching software can make them; the Slide Scanner page says who makes such software now.

想给自己的课堂做这样的切片？一台带相机的显微镜加上拼接软件就能做；切片扫描仪页面写了现在谁在做这类软件。
