Why it exists

For students without a microscope

X-WOW wanted students who have no microscope to be able to look at real slides. In 2019 it set out to do that for a group of students in Nigeria.

The first attempt failed. Whole-slide images were shared, but the viewer needed a far more powerful computer than the students had. About five months later, a viewer that runs in any web browser solved it: the whole collection went online, readable on any device.

Credit

A. M. F., then a microbiology student in Abuja: the reason this exists. As the old site put it, without that help none of this would have happened. The same student went on to found the X-WOW Kids Science Club. (Credited by initials: X-WOW names no private person without their permission.)

Where the slides live

Off the old site, still online

The scans were never stored on the old website. They sit on EasyZoom under the X-WOW account, and the old pages only framed them, which is why they survive the move to this site. The one dependency left is that account: if it ever closes, these links stop working.

Want slides like these for your own class? A microscope with a camera and stitching software can make them; the Slide Scanner page says who makes such software now.
