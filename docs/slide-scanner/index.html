X-WOW Slide ScannerX-WOW 切片扫描仪

For a while, X-WOW offered a small kit that turned an ordinary lab microscope into a whole-slide scanner: a microscope camera plus scanning software. The software came from Microvisioneer, a German company. It was offered under the X-WOW name as part of a past collaboration, and X-WOW no longer offers it.

有一段时间，X-WOW 提供过一套小小的组合，能把普通实验室显微镜变成全切片扫描仪：一台显微镜相机加一套扫描软件。软件来自德国公司 Microvisioneer。它当时以 X-WOW 的名义提供，是一段过去的合作；现在 X-WOW 已不再提供。

X-WOW has no current partnership with Microvisioneer.

X-WOW 目前和 Microvisioneer 没有任何合作。

Scanner status: retired here, alive at the source.

扫描仪状态：这里已退役，源头还活着。

How it worked它是怎么工作的

Move the slide by hand; the software stitches as you go用手移动切片，软件边看边拼

  1. Fit the camera to the microscope's C-mount port and connect it to a computer by USB 3.把相机装到显微镜的 C 接口上，用 USB 3 连到电脑。
  2. Choose the objective in the software and balance brightness and colour on an empty patch of glass.在软件里选好物镜，对着一块空白玻璃把亮度和颜色调平衡。
  3. Move the slide in overlapping stripes. The live view grows into one stitched image; if the software loses its place, you move back slowly and carry on.把切片一条条、互相重叠地移动。实时画面会长成一整张拼接图；软件跟丢了，就慢慢移回去接着扫。
  4. Save the result as a pyramidal TIFF or SVS file and open it in a viewer such as the open-source QuPath.把结果存成金字塔结构的 TIFF 或 SVS 文件，用开源的 QuPath 等软件打开。

From the old X-WOW setup guide. As an example, it gave a 5 × 5 mm area scanned at 40× as roughly 90 MB at 90% compression quality.

以上摘自旧的 X-WOW 安装指南。指南里举的例子：40 倍下扫描 5 × 5 毫米的区域，按 90% 压缩质量保存，大约 90 MB。

Why it mattered为什么重要

A dedicated slide scanner costs more than many small labs can pay. Doing the moving by hand let a lab turn the microscope it already had into a scanner, and share whole slides with colleagues anywhere.

一台专用切片扫描仪，很多小实验室买不起。改成手动移动切片，实验室就能把已有的显微镜变成扫描仪，再把整张切片分享给任何地方的同行。

Malawi, 2019马拉维，2019 年

In March 2019, X-WOW ran a fundraiser and bought a manual whole-slide imaging system, costing under £5,000: a "magic wand" for Prof Elizabeth Molyneux's paediatric cancer work in Malawi, so slides could be reviewed together with colleagues 7,400 miles away.

2019 年 3 月，X-WOW 发起众筹，买下一套手动全切片成像系统（不到 5,000 英镑），作为送给 Elizabeth Molyneux 教授在马拉维儿童肿瘤工作的“魔法棒”，让切片可以和 7,400 英里外的同行一起看。

Who makes it now现在谁在做

Microvisioneer still makes this kind of software. On its website, its manual scanning product is called mvSlide: it upgrades an existing microscope that has a suitable camera into a manual slide scanner, stitching the image in real time. It also offers InstaSlide for sharing zoomable virtual slides and mvLive for sharing live camera images.

Microvisioneer 现在仍在做这类软件。按它官网的介绍，手动扫描的产品叫 mvSlide：给已有的、配了合适相机的显微镜升级，变成手动切片扫描仪，图像实时拼接。它还有用来分享可缩放虚拟切片的 InstaSlide，以及分享实时相机画面的 mvLive。

Product names and details are theirs and may change; their own website, microvisioneer.com, is the authority. Would you rather build an instrument yourself? Start with SWELL, the first invention on this site.

产品名称和细节以他们自己的官网 microvisioneer.com 为准，随时可能更新。更想自己动手造仪器？从本站的第一个发明 SWELL 开始。
