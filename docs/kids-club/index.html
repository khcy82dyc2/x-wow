Abuja, Nigeria · 2019–2020 · past project

X-WOW Kids Science Club

University students in Abuja started a science club around a microscope, books and slides that X-WOW sent, to show schoolchildren the microbial world. X-WOW funded it. The students ran it.

What happened

One microscope, one club

In 2019 X-WOW sent a microbiology student in Abuja (A. M. F.) a microscope, children's books about microbes, slides and other teaching items. With them the student founded the X-WOW Kids Science Club in Abuja.

Sister club · Abuja, Nigeria

Future Vet Pathologists Club

A second club, also funded by X-WOW, run by veterinary students in Abuja. They tutored high-school and pre-university students in diagnostic veterinary pathology: how a microscope is used to find disease.

Its founder had received one of the re-homed microscopes from Back to Shine. In June 2020 the club co-organised a remote neuropathology lecture with a veterinary pathologist from a UK research institute, and its event page listed 878 more registered guests beside those pictured (registration was open to anyone).
