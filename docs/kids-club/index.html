X-WOW Kids Science ClubX-WOW 儿童科学俱乐部

University students in Abuja carried a donated microscope into local primary and secondary schools, so pupils who had never seen one could look at the microbial world for themselves. X-WOW funded it. The students ran it.

阿布贾的大学生把一台捐赠的显微镜带进当地的小学和中学，让从没见过显微镜的孩子亲眼看看微生物的世界。X-WOW 出钱，学生们出力。

What happened发生了什么

One microscope, many classrooms一台显微镜，很多间教室

In 2019 X-WOW sent Akpan M. Friday, a microbiology student at the University of Abuja, a microscope, children's books about microbes, slides and other teaching items. With them he founded the X-WOW Kids Science Club in Abuja.

2019 年，X-WOW 给阿布贾大学微生物学专业的学生 Akpan M. Friday 寄去一台显微镜、给孩子看的微生物书、切片和其他教具。他用这些东西在阿布贾创办了 X-WOW 儿童科学俱乐部。

The team visited primary and secondary schools across the Federal Capital Territory. They showed pupils how a microscope works, taught them about microbes and ran microbe-themed activities. Their aim was microbial literacy: children growing up knowing how microbes shape everyday health. In his letter to X-WOW, Akpan reported that the team had reached over 100 pupils in its first months.

团队走访了联邦首都区的多所小学和中学：教孩子们用显微镜、讲微生物、做和微生物有关的小活动。目标是“微生物素养”，让孩子从小就明白微生物怎样影响日常健康。Akpan 在写给 X-WOW 的信里说，头几个月里团队已经接触了 100 多名学生。

Future Vet Pathologists Club未来兽医病理学家俱乐部

A second club, also funded by X-WOW, run by veterinary students at the University of Abuja. They tutored high-school and pre-university students in diagnostic veterinary pathology: how a microscope is used to find disease.

第二个俱乐部，同样由 X-WOW 资助，由阿布贾大学的兽医学学生运营。他们给高中生和大学预科生讲兽医诊断病理学：怎样用显微镜找出疾病。

Its founder had received one of the re-homed microscopes from Back to Shine. In June 2020 the club co-organised a remote neuropathology lecture with Dr Francesca Chianini of the Moredun Research Institute, and almost 900 veterinary students in Africa registered for it.

它的创办人就是旧仪器再发光里一台显微镜的接收人。2020 年 6 月，俱乐部和莫雷登研究所的 Francesca Chianini 博士一起办了一场神经病理学远程讲座，非洲有近 900 名兽医学生报名。
