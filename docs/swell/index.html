Invention #1 · what it is发明一号 · 这是什么

Make the sample bigger, then look with cheap glass先把样本泡大，再用便宜镜头看

SWELL is a design for an automated expansion microscope, meant to be cheap; whether it is waits until every part is priced. A small piece of supermarket chicken breast is anchored inside a gel that swells in water, so the muscle is physically enlarged, first by about 4–4.8× and later by 10×. A small 3D-printed microscope then scans the swollen gel, tile by tile.

SWELL 是一台全自动“膨胀显微镜”的设计，目标是低成本；到底便不便宜，要等每个零件都查到价格才知道。把一小块超市鸡胸肉固定在遇水会膨胀的凝胶里，肌肉就被实打实地放大：先放大约 4–4.8×，以后做到 10×。然后由一台 3D 打印的小显微镜把泡大的凝胶一格一格扫下来。

01

Anchor and swell锚定，泡大

The muscle's proteins are tied into a sodium-acrylate gel. Heat and detergent loosen the tissue, then the gel swells in water and pulls the proteins apart, ideally by the same amount in every direction.

先把肌肉里的蛋白质“拴”进聚丙烯酸钠凝胶，再用加热和去污剂把组织松开。凝胶在水里膨胀，把蛋白质往外拉开；理想情况下，各个方向放大得一样多。

02

Image成像

A printed flexure stage moves the gel over an objective and a mono camera. Hundreds of tiles and several focus planes are stitched into one image of the whole gel.

一个 3D 打印的柔性载物台带着凝胶在物镜和黑白相机上方移动，几百个视野、好几层焦面拼成一整块凝胶的大图。

03

Let the meat check itself让肉自己当尺子

Muscle comes with rulers. Each myofibril is a chain of sarcomeres, about 1.69 µm long in supermarket breast. Comparing the stripe spacing of each long myofibril before and after expansion would show how much that myofibril lengthened. Whether that equals the gel's expansion there is untested: dense protein structures can expand less than the gel around them (Damstra et al. 2022; Sheard 2021). Put together, these would give the strain map: a picture of how evenly the fibrils expanded across the gel.

肌肉自带尺子。每根肌原纤维由一节节肌节串成，超市鸡胸肉的肌节长约 1.69 µm。比较每一根足够长的肌原纤维膨胀前后的条纹间距，可以推算出这根肌原纤维伸长了多少。它是否等于凝胶在那里的膨胀，还没有测试过：致密的蛋白质结构可能比周围的凝胶胀得少（Damstra 等 2022；Sheard 2021）。把它们拼起来，就能画出应变图：各处的肌原纤维胀得匀不匀，一眼可见。

Why it works为什么行得通

Three cheap ideas三个便宜的点子

The project's design rule: chemistry does the resolution work, so the optics can stay cheap. More expansion with a modest lens beats expensive glass.

项目的设计原则：分辨率交给化学，光学就可以便宜。多胀几倍、配一个普通镜头，胜过昂贵的玻璃。

1 · Grow the sample, not the glass1 · 放大样本，而不是升级镜头

A lens cannot separate two points closer than about 0.61·λ/NA. For the 0.65 NA objective at 520 nm that is 488 nm. Expansion moves the molecules apart before the light ever reaches the lens, and the project's rule of thumb is effective resolution ≈ optical resolution ÷ E. In original-specimen units that is about 108 nm at 4.5×, and 79 nm at 10× with the 0.40 NA objective.

镜头分不开比约 0.61·λ/NA 更近的两个点。数值孔径 0.65 的物镜在 520 nm 波长下，这个距离是 488 nm。膨胀在光到达镜头之前就把分子拉开了；项目的经验公式是：有效分辨率 ≈ 光学分辨率 ÷ E。换算回原样本尺度，4.5× 时约 108 nm；换数值孔径 0.40 的物镜、放大 10× 时约 79 nm。

The lens's diffraction limit does not change; the specimen gets bigger. These are paper numbers, and still not electron microscopy: you only see what you label, and the chemistry can make its own artefacts.

Who got there first: Zhang, Chang and colleagues paired expansion with a webcam mini-microscope in 2016 ("expansion mini-microscopy"), reaching, after expansion, a resolution comparable to a benchtop microscope's on unexpanded samples, at a hundredth to a thousandth of the price. SWELL builds on their idea.

谁先做到的：Zhang、Chang 等人在 2016 年就把膨胀显微和网络摄像头做的迷你显微镜结合起来（"膨胀迷你显微"），膨胀之后，用百分之一到千分之一的价格，达到了与台式显微镜看未膨胀样品相当的分辨率。SWELL 是在他们的思路上往前走。

镜头的衍射极限一点没变，变大的是样本。这些都是纸面计算，也远不是电镜：只能看到被标记的东西，化学步骤本身还可能制造假象。

2 · A ruler grown into the meat2 · 长在肉里的尺子

E = SLpost ÷ SLpre

Sarcomere spacing is measured on the same myofibril before and after expansion. The project's notes call the period blur-invariant: blur softens each stripe but leaves the spacing alone, so the ratio survives cheap optics. The before-image should need no label: differential phase contrast with four oblique LEDs is predicted to show the stripes (the project's choice after the physics check; untested), with crossed polarisers as a cross-check.

在同一根肌原纤维上，膨胀前后各量一次肌节间距。项目笔记说这个周期“不受模糊影响”：模糊只让条纹变软，间距不变，所以便宜镜头也能用这个比值。膨胀前那张图应该不用染色：按预测，用四个斜射 LED 做差分相衬（DPC）能看到条纹（这是物理核算后项目选定的方式，未经测试），正交偏振光作为交叉核对。

It replaced the textbook A-band ruler, which needs sarcomeres long enough to show an I-band: only about 13% of supermarket breast at 4×.

它取代了教科书里的 A 带尺子。那把尺子要求肌节长到能看见 I 带，超市鸡胸肉在 4× 下只有约 13% 合格。

Who got there first: Chen, Tillberg and Boyden (2015), who introduced expansion microscopy, imaged the same cells before and after expansion and measured how far lengths moved (under 1% of the distance in cultured cells, 2–4% in brain slices). Sheard and colleagues (2019) used the periodicity of sarcomeres and the fixed width of Z-discs as intrinsic reporters of local gel expansion in heart cells, and Sheard's 2021 thesis found the sarcomere factor lower than the gel's. A 2022 thesis by Migliore expanded isolated rabbit myofibrils, imaged them without labels and used the A-band as the ruler. VISTA (Miao, Lin, Qian and Wei, 2022) measured expansion by comparing label-free images of the same structures before and after. What SWELL adds is pairing each myofibril's label-free period before expansion with its own period after.

谁先做到的：发明膨胀显微术的 Chen、Tillberg 与 Boyden（2015）就在膨胀前后拍同一批细胞，量出长度偏移了多少（培养细胞里不到距离的 1%，脑切片里 2–4%）。Sheard 等人（2019）在心肌细胞里，用肌节的周期和 Z 盘的固定宽度作为凝胶局部膨胀的内在指示；Sheard 2021 年的学位论文发现，肌节算出的倍数比凝胶的低。Migliore 2022 年的学位论文膨胀过分离的兔肌原纤维，不染色成像，并用 A 带当尺子。VISTA（Miao、Lin、Qian 与 Wei，2022）通过比较同一结构膨胀前后的免标记图像来测膨胀。SWELL 加的是：把每一根肌原纤维膨胀前的免标记周期，和它自己膨胀后的周期配对。

3 · Cheap glass, just enough of it3 · 便宜镜头，刚好够用

Motic PL 40X/0.65 and PL 20X/0.40 plan objectives, one 100 mm achromat 25 mm behind the objective, and a mono IMX296 global-shutter camera (1456 × 1088 px, 3.45 µm) at £49.87. Together: 17.8× and 0.194 µm pixels, which is 1.03× Nyquist for the 4× track and 0.84× for the 10× track.

Motic PL 40X/0.65 和 PL 20X/0.40 平场物镜，物镜后 25 mm 处放一片 100 mm 消色差透镜，再配一台 £49.87 的 IMX296 黑白全局快门相机（1456 × 1088 px, 3.45 µm）。合起来是 17.8×、每像素 0.194 µm：4× 路线达到奈奎斯特采样的 1.03×，10× 路线为 0.84×。

The stock OpenFlexure lens would undersample (0.66× Nyquist), and a colour sensor wastes signal, so neither is used. The stage would be a fork of the open OpenFlexure Microscope v7, run by a Raspberry Pi 5 and a Pico 2.

OpenFlexure 自带的镜头会欠采样（只有奈奎斯特的 0.66×），彩色传感器又浪费信号，所以都不用。载物台将改自开源的 OpenFlexure 显微镜 v7，由树莓派 5 和 Pico 2 控制。

Built on open work: the OpenFlexure Microscope (Collins et al., 2020; build docs), expansion microscopy (Chen, Tillberg & Boyden, 2015), expansion mini-microscopy (Zhang, Chang et al., 2016), sarcomeres as intrinsic expansion reporters (Sheard et al., 2019), U-ExM (Gambarotto et al., 2019), pan-ExM (M'Saad & Bewersdorf, 2020) and TREx (Damstra et al., 2022). Every source, with what SWELL took from it, is listed under credits.

站在这些公开成果的肩膀上：OpenFlexure 显微镜（Collins 等，2020；搭建文档）、膨胀显微术（Chen、Tillberg 与 Boyden，2015）、膨胀迷你显微（Zhang、Chang 等，2016）、以肌节作为膨胀的内在指示（Sheard 等，2019）、U-ExM（Gambarotto 等，2019）、pan-ExM（M'Saad 与 Bewersdorf，2020）和 TREx（Damstra 等，2022）。所有出处，以及 SWELL 从中借了什么，都列在致谢里。

The numbers, honestly数字，老实说

What the simulation says, including where it fails模拟说了什么，包括没做好的地方

Every result here comes from synthetic gels with a known truth. Synthetic data tests the analysis pipeline against our own assumptions, not the chemistry: a real build would have to repeat every check on real gels.

这里的每个结果都来自“已知标准答案”的合成凝胶。合成数据只能拿我们自己的假设去检验分析流程，检验不了化学；真造出来以后，每一项检查都要在真凝胶上重做一遍。

Read this first. This section is version 1, measured on gels from our own generator. The only test built by a separate AI agent's generator, red-team gel S3 (see Tested harder), went worse: per-myofibril axial error 1.8% RMS with 7.5% beyond ±2%, a transverse bias of −17.8%, and 0% of the under-expanded patch flagged. Treat everything below as development results.

先看这里。这一节是第一版，测的是我们自己的生成器造的凝胶。唯一一块由另一个 AI 智能体的生成器造的考试凝胶 S3（见更严的考试）结果更差：每根肌原纤维轴向误差均方根 1.8%，7.5% 超过 ±2%；横向偏差 −17.8%；膨胀不足的区域只标出了 0%。下面的一切都请当作开发阶段的结果来看。

The first estimate was too optimistic, and the simulation said so第一次估计太乐观了，是模拟自己揭穿的

simulated模拟

The first desk calculation said one myofibril segment of 10 sarcomeres gives its expansion to 0.21–0.35% (RMS), for assumed before-image contrast of 5–50%. It assumed perfectly regular sarcomeres. Real ones vary by about 3% from one to the next, and under that jitter (and at some sarcomere lengths) the original estimator (a free-frequency fit) locked onto side-lobes: 10–17% of myofibrils came out wrong by 7–19%. The first full whole-gel simulation failed 15 checks.

第一次纸面计算说：一段含 10 个肌节的肌原纤维，放大倍数能测准到 0.21–0.35%（均方根误差），前提是膨胀前图像的对比度在 5–50% 之间。可这个计算假设肌节长得完全一样。真实肌节一节和一节之间差约 3%，原来的估计方法（自由频率拟合）一遇到这种抖动（以及某些肌节长度）就会锁到旁瓣上：10–17% 的肌原纤维被算错 7–19%。第一次整块凝胶模拟因此挂掉了 15 项检查。

The fix is the Z-disc ladder: the Z-discs in the expanded image define a sarcomere-by-sarcomere coordinate, and the before-image is fitted on that ladder, so the jitter is carried along instead of fought. Segments need at least 8 sarcomeres. Result: 0.3–0.6% RMS per myofibril across simulation runs, 0.32–0.48% across the four gels of the final run and 0.35% on the blind gel, with at most 0.48% of myofibrils off by more than ±2%.

修正办法叫 Z 盘梯子：用膨胀后图像里的 Z 盘标出一节一节的肌节坐标，再沿着这把“梯子”去拟合膨胀前的图像，抖动被顺着带走，而不是硬扛。每段至少要 8 个肌节。结果：各轮模拟中每根肌原纤维的误差为 0.3–0.6%（均方根），最终一轮四块凝胶为 0.32–0.48%，盲测凝胶为 0.35%；偏差超过 ±2% 的肌原纤维最多占 0.48%。

The error bar went up, and the simulation itself raised it.

误差范围变大了，而且是模拟自己调大的。

Three of the gels (S0, S1, S2) are development gels, because the detectors were fixed while looking at S1; S1b was our own blind gel, and S3 has since replaced it as the red-team test. Coverage: about half the myofibrils carry the axial ruler覆盖率：约一半肌原纤维能用轴向尺子

Every myofibril gets the transverse (width) ruler and the landmark (position) ruler. Only segments of at least 8 sarcomeres get the axial period ratio: 665 of 1,200 (55%) in the blind gel and 626 of 1,200 (52%) in the development gels. In real meat this would depend on how gently the tissue is homogenised.

每根肌原纤维都能用横向（宽度）尺子和地标（位置）尺子。但只有至少 8 个肌节长的片段才能算轴向周期比：盲测凝胶的 1,200 根里有 665 根（55%），开发凝胶为 626 根（52%）。换成真肉，这取决于打碎组织时有多温柔。

Transverse isotropy and the shape-aware ruler横向各向同性与“认形状”的尺子

A gel can swell evenly along a myofibril and unevenly across it. So the pass criteria include transverse isotropy, Eax ÷ Etr − 1 within ±3% (95% CI, n ≥ 200), and the axial and transverse factors are always reported separately. On the blind gel the transverse factor per myofibril is 0.00% ± 0.50% (bias ± SD).

凝胶可能顺着肌原纤维胀得很匀，横着却不匀。所以合格标准里加了横向各向同性：Eax ÷ Etr − 1 落在 ±3% 以内（95% 置信区间，n ≥ 200），轴向和横向的放大倍数永远分开报告。盲测凝胶上，每根肌原纤维的横向放大倍数偏差为 0.00% ± 0.50%（偏差 ± 标准差）。

The width ruler is the fragile one: it needs a model of each cross-section. A boxy cross-section fitted as an ellipse is off by −9.0% to −15.2%, depending on the before-image method. So the pipeline measures each myofibril's cross-section shape from the expanded z-stack, and the bias drops to ≤ 0.04%.

宽度尺子最娇气：它得知道每根纤维的横截面长什么样。把方形截面当椭圆来拟合，会差 −9.0% 到 −15.2%（取决于膨胀前图像的拍法）。所以流程改成从膨胀后的 z 叠层里直接量每根纤维的截面形状，偏差降到 ≤ 0.04%。

Blur calibration still matters. If the before-image blur is calibrated 5% too wide, the bias is 1.05–3.23%, so the first gate calibrates it to ±2%. At that target the worst bias in our own model is 1.09%. Red-team gel S3 showed the model is not enough: a blur shape the ruler does not model biased it by −17.8%, with the right blur widths or not.

模糊标定照样要紧。膨胀前图像的模糊如果标宽了 5%，偏差就是 1.05–3.23%；所以第一道关卡要求标定到 ±2%。在这个精度下，按我们自己的模型最坏偏差是 1.09%。红队凝胶 S3 证明这个模型不够：一种标尺没建模的模糊形状让它偏了 −17.8%，模糊宽度给没给对都一样。

On development gel S2, a planted 2% anisotropy is recovered as −2.12% [−2.37%, −1.87%] (truth −2.03%), and a planted gel stretch of 2.00% along 23° is found by the orientation test as 2.03% along 23°.

在开发凝胶 S2 上，埋入的 2% 各向异性被测成 −2.12% [−2.37%, −1.87%]（真值 −2.03%）；埋入的整体拉伸（沿 23° 方向 2.00%）被方向检验测成沿 23° 方向 2.03%。

Finding a suitable lab, anywhere在任何地方找合适的实验室

The gel chemistry belongs only in an equipped, supervised laboratory that works under its own safety rules. We name no labs here: this is the list of what to ask one for. Nobody has been contacted about SWELL.

Measure a chicken with a laser用激光量一量鸡肉

You don't need SWELL to measure a chicken's sarcomeres. Muscle is striped every couple of micrometres, and the stripes act as a diffraction grating. Shine a cheap laser pointer through a thin thread of raw chicken breast and the beam splits into spots; how far apart they land gives the sarcomere length (SL).   2. Cut (adult): a strip about the size of a matchstick, along the grain; the fine lines you can see in the meat run along the fibres.
  3. Tease (adult): pull it apart lengthways with the knife tip or a cocktail stick until the thread is only a few fibres thick, thin enough to see light through. Thinner gives sharper spots.
  4. Sandwich (adult): lay the thread straight between the two slides, note which way the fibres run, and hold the slides in the peg or clip, not fingers. Turn the sandwich so the fibres run level (left to right): the side spots then land at table height, not up towards faces.
  5. Shine (adult). Darken the room and switch on. Put the sandwich in the beam with the glass facing the pointer, so the beam crosses the fibres at right angles. Tilt the slides slightly so the glass reflects down onto the table, not back at you.

The maths算法

The sarcomeres repeat every SL along the fibre, so light only leaves at angles θ where neighbouring sarcomeres send it out in step (order m = 1, 2, …):

肌节沿纤维每隔一个 SL 重复一次，所以光只会在某些角度 θ 射出：在那些角度上，相邻肌节发出的光步调一致（级次 m = 1, 2, …）：

SL · sin θm = m · λ  ⇒  SL = m · λ / sin θm

You can't measure θ directly, but you can measure x (centre to spot, on the card) and L (meat to card):

θ 没法直接量，但能量 x（卡纸上中心到光点）和 L（肉到卡纸）：

sin θ = x / √(x² + L²)  ⇒  SL = λ · √(x² + L²) / x (first order, m = 1)（一级，m = 1）

Worked example: λ = 650 nm, L = 20 cm, and you read x = 83 mm. Then SL = λ√(x² + L²)/x = 1.70 µm. Put x and L in the same unit (both in millimetres: L = 200 mm) and λ in micrometres (0.650), and the answer comes out in micrometres.

算个例子：λ = 650 nm，L = 20 cm，你量到 x = 83 mm。那么 SL = λ√(x² + L²)/x = 1.70 µm。x 和 L 要用同一个单位（都用毫米：L = 200 mm），λ 用微米（0.650），算出来的就是微米。

Words on this page本页用到的词

A short glossary简明词汇表

Sarcomere
One repeating unit of a muscle fibre, about 1.69 µm long in supermarket chicken breast. A muscle contracts by shortening them.
Myofibril
A thin thread inside a muscle cell, made of sarcomeres joined end to end. SWELL uses them as rulers.
Z-disc
The thin line where one sarcomere meets the next.
Expansion factor (E)
How many times bigger the gel made the sample: a length after expansion divided by the same length before.
Eax and Etr
The expansion factor along a myofibril (axial) and across it (transverse).
Anisotropy
Growing more in one direction than another. On this page it is Eax ÷ Etr − 1.
Non-affine
A distortion that no single stretch, shift and turn of the whole gel can explain, such as a tear or a fold.
PSF
Point spread function: the blurred spot a microscope makes of a single point of light. The narrower it is, the finer the detail you can see.
Nyquist sampling
Taking at least two pixels across the finest detail you want to see. With fewer, that detail is lost.
Strehl ratio
How bright the peak of a focused spot is compared with a perfect lens's, from 0 to 1; 1 means no aberration. On this page it is computed in simulation, relative to the gel's surface.
q
The number the label-free stripe test hangs on: stripe contrast × √(photons per sample). The higher q is, the more precisely the stripe spacing can be read.
DPC
Differential phase contrast: lighting a clear specimen from one side, then the other, and comparing the two images, so thin transparent structures show up without a stain.
Clock tile
One patch of the gel imaged again every 50 tiles during a scan, so that slow swelling during the scan can be measured and taken out.
Overview camera
A small camera that photographs the whole gel, to measure its overall size before and after expansion.
CMR
Carcinogenic, mutagenic or toxic for reproduction: hazard classes some gel chemicals carry. UK law restricts selling them to the public.
G0
The first gate in SWELL's plan: a complete, checked design on paper, before any part is bought or any gel is made. SWELL hasn't passed it.
Simulated, synthetic
Made by a computer from a model, not measured on anything real.
肌节
肌纤维里一节一节重复的单位，超市鸡胸肉里长约 1.69 µm。肌肉收缩，就是肌节在变短。
肌原纤维
肌肉细胞里的细丝，由肌节一节接一节串成。SWELL 把它们当尺子用。
Z 盘
一个肌节和下一个肌节交界处的那条细线。
膨胀倍数（E）
凝胶把样品放大了多少倍：膨胀后的某段长度除以膨胀前的同一段长度。
Eax 和 Etr
沿着肌原纤维方向（轴向）和横着它的方向（横向）的膨胀倍数。
各向异性
往一个方向胀得比另一个方向多。本页里它是 Eax ÷ Etr − 1。
非仿射
整块凝胶任何一次统一的拉伸、平移和旋转都解释不了的形变，比如裂开或折叠。
点扩散函数（PSF）
显微镜把一个光点拍成的那个模糊小斑。它越窄，能看清的细节越细。
奈奎斯特采样
要看清的最细的细节上，至少要占两个像素。少了，这个细节就丢了。
斯特列尔比
聚焦光斑的峰值亮度和完美镜头相比是多少，在 0 到 1 之间；1 表示没有像差。本页里它是在模拟中、相对凝胶表面算出来的。
q
免标记条纹检验所依赖的那个数：条纹反差 × √（每个采样点的光子数）。q 越大，条纹间距读得越准。
差分相衬（DPC）
先从一侧、再从另一侧照亮透明的样品，比较两张图，让薄而透明的结构不染色也能显出来。
时钟视野
扫描时每拍 50 个视野就回去重拍一次的同一块凝胶，用来测出并扣掉扫描过程中缓慢的膨胀。
全景相机
拍下整块凝胶的小相机，用来量凝胶膨胀前后的整体大小。
CMR
致癌、致突变或有生殖毒性：一些凝胶化学品带有的危害类别。英国法律限制把它们卖给公众。
G0
SWELL 计划里的第一道关卡：在买任何零件、做任何凝胶之前，有一份完整、经过检查的纸面设计。SWELL 还没有通过它。
模拟、合成
电脑按模型算出来的，不是在真实的东西上测出来的。
