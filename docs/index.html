Origin起源

From Pathology Without Borders to borderless invention从无国界病理，到无国界发明

Read the origin story读一读起源

X-WOW began as the Pathology Without Borders Initiative. From December 2018 its founder found microscopes new homes with people who needed them, seven by the old site's count, and began asking labs for the retired ones gathering dust. In 2019, kind people chipped in for a low-cost slide scanner, so doctors treating children with cancer in Malawi could share slides with colleagues 7,400 miles away. A microbiology student in Abuja told the founder that most students around him never touch a real microscope. So scanned biology slides went online, named after him, and he went on to start a kids' science club. When classrooms closed in 2020, scientists gave free online lectures to students across Africa and beyond, part-funded by the Royal College of Pathologists' Public Engagement Grant.

X-WOW 最早叫“无国界病理”计划。从 2018 年 12 月起，X-WOW 的创办人给显微镜找新家，送到需要的人手里，按旧网站的统计一共七台；也开始向实验室讨要那些在角落吃灰的退役显微镜。2019 年，一群好心人凑钱买了一套便宜的切片扫描系统，让马拉维给孩子看癌症的医生，能把切片发给 7,400 英里外的同行一起看。阿布贾一位学微生物的大学生告诉创办人，他身边的同学大多从没碰过真正的显微镜。于是扫描好的生物切片被放到网上，用他的名字命名；后来他自己办起了儿童科学俱乐部。2020 年教室关门，科学家们为非洲和世界各地的学生开了免费网课，部分经费来自英国皇家病理学家学会的公众参与基金。

The lesson: a microscope in a cupboard helps nobody, and neither does software that needs a powerful computer. Tools have to travel light and cost little. So the mission widens. Not just pathology, and not just old equipment: ways to see more closely and to make the tools, designed in the open and given away. SWELL is the first.

学到的是：放在柜子里的显微镜帮不了任何人，要高配电脑才能跑的软件也一样。工具得轻便、便宜，才走得远。所以使命要放宽：不只是病理，也不只是旧设备，而是看得更近的办法和做工具的办法，公开设计，免费送出。SWELL 是第一个。

Inputs, 2018–2020: seven microscopes, one scanner, a season of free lectures. The numbers were small; the lesson was not.

2018–2020 年的投入：七台显微镜、一台扫描仪、一季免费讲座。数字不大，学到的东西不小。
